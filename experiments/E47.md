## E47: is the E33 "truncation" story an EFFORT artifact? (2026-08-16, pre-registered before any run)

**Why this exists.** `normalize_path` truncated at 8,192, truncated at 24,576,
and at 100,000 produced 51,845 tokens of reasoning and no code. Every one of
those observations is at `xhigh` or at the template default, which resolves to
`xhigh`. **The five E33-capped tasks have never been run at `medium`**, and the
E42 coding row measured `medium` as strictly better than every other level on
the expert set - 7/8, zero truncations, in fewer tokens than `low`.

**Config.** `tools/run_coding_eval.py --task-set core --only
parse_semver,normalize_path,roman_roundtrip,word_break,eval_expr`,
`--thinking medium`, `--ctx 32768`, `--max-tokens 24576`, b10435,
temperature 0. **Deliberately the E33 RE-RUN 2 config with ONE variable
changed**, so any difference is attributable to effort and nothing else.

24,576 rather than 100,000 on purpose: the question is whether `medium`
converges inside a budget `xhigh` could not, and giving it four times as much
would confound the comparison it exists to make.

**Predictions. Bands set high, per the standing correction - mine have sat
below the measured result six times in this programme.**

1. **At least 4 of the 5 tasks return `finish_reason: stop`** at `medium`
   within 24,576. At `xhigh` three of them cap at that budget.
2. **`normalize_path` produces code** - any outcome other than `no_code` or
   `truncated`. It need not be correct; the claim is that it converges.
3. **Total tokens for the five tasks at `medium` is under 60,000**, against
   `xhigh` spending 24,576 on three of them alone plus 51,845 on
   `normalize_path` at the larger budget.
4. **At least 3 of 5 PASS.** This is the strongest claim and the one I would
   least defend.

**Falsifier: if `normalize_path` still fails to produce code at `medium`, the
effort hypothesis is dead for this task** and it is simply beyond the model.
That is a clean answer too, and it would mean the routing guide should say so
rather than implying a setting fixes it.

**What turns on this.** If prediction 2 holds, then a defect this repository
has recorded as a token-budget problem since July - and re-ran twice to fix -
was a reasoning-effort setting the whole time, and the E33 findings need a note
saying so.

## E37: professional-services knowledge work, graded WITHOUT a judge (2026-08-15, pre-registered before any run)

**Why this exists.** Every knowledge-work result in results/ (F34, F35, F37)
is office drafting scored by a frontier model acting as judge. The ruler is
therefore itself an unmeasured instrument, and "local matches frontier on
knowledge work" is a claim resting on a cloud model's opinion. This
experiment removes the judge entirely: twenty questions over a seeded
engagement pack, every answer checked by string or numeric match.

The task shape comes from what a KPMG/EY/Deloitte/BCG deployment is actually
evaluated on, which is narrower and far more testable than "knowledge work":
extraction with a citation, using the amended figure rather than the
superseded one, arithmetic over a table, noticing that two documents
disagree, and knowing what the pack does not say.

**Config.** `tools/run_ps_eval.py`; corpus `spikes/ps-eval/corpus.py` (six
documents, 10,741 chars, ~2.7K tokens); questions and graders
`spikes/ps-eval/questions.py`. llama-server b10435, `-c 16384`, `-np 1` (so
-c is the per-request budget, F20), temperature 0 (F59), `max_tokens 4096`.
One request per question, pack resent each time - batching would let one
answer leak into another. Rule 3 offload gate asserted and FAILING on zero
evidence. Cloud arms via OpenRouter/Mistral share the $40 ceiling.

**This is NOT a long-context test.** The pack is ~2.7K tokens. Length is
E35's question; this one is about professional judgement over several
documents. Do not quote it as evidence about context length.

**Two design choices that materially move the scores, declared up front:**

1. The prompt STATES that the most recent amendment governs. Withholding it
   would conflate not knowing that amendments supersede with not finding the
   amendment, and only the second bears on the routing decision. This makes
   the supersession items EASIER and any failure correspondingly more damning.
2. The answer must be two lines, `ANSWER:` and `CITATION:`. A response that
   does not carry the form is recorded as `format_error`, NOT as a wrong
   answer - F20's lesson, that a mechanical failure scored as a capability
   failure is how a benchmark lies.

**Arms.** Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M (subject); Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 (the
workhorse incumbent for this slot, the control); `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` on
OpenRouter (the same model unquantised - isolates what our Q4_K_M + Vulkan
stack costs); one cheap cloud arm as a floor.

**The graders are validated, not assumed.** `tools/validate_ps_eval.py`
drives them with four synthetic respondents: a perfect set scores 20/20 with
20/20 citations; a respondent reading only the base contract produces exactly
4 `stale_value`; one that invents figures produces exactly 4 `over_claim`; one
ignoring the format produces 20 `format_error`. Run and passing at time of
registration.

**Predictions (registered before any arm runs). The FIELD is named in each.**

1. **`over_claim_rate` (fabricated answers / 4 unanswerable items) is the
   discriminating metric, and Qwen3.8-Q4_K_M scores WORSE than the
   unquantised cloud arm of the same model.** Registered bands: local Q4_K_M
   **0.50-1.00** (2-4 of 4 fabricated); `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` cloud **0.00-0.50**.
   If the local Q4 matches the cloud arm at 0.00-0.25, that is the strongest
   on-prem result available tonight and the quantisation costs nothing that
   matters here.
2. **`correct` total, out of 20.** Qwen3.8 **11-16**; workhorse control
   **9-14**; `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` cloud **14-18**.
3. **`stale_values` (of 4 supersession items).** Qwen3.8 **0-2**; workhorse
   **1-3**. The instruction that amendments govern is given, so anything
   above 2 means the model did not carry the amendment chain rather than
   did not know the convention.
4. **`citation_valid` out of 20 is LOWER than `correct` for every arm.**
   Getting the answer and naming where it came from are different
   capabilities, and citation is the one a firm cannot do without.
5. **`computation` category (4 items) is the weakest for the local arms,
   at 1-3 of 4.** Exact arithmetic over a table is the classic LLM failure
   and nothing in this stack fixes it.

**Not predicted:** which individual questions fail. Naming them after seeing
the set would be theatre; the category bands above are the falsifiable part.

**Falsifier for the whole experiment:** if any arm scores `format_error` on
more than 4 of 20, that arm's content scores are not interpretable and must
not be quoted - the run is measuring instruction-following, not knowledge
work, and the prompt needs fixing before any verdict rests on it.

**Amendments:** AMENDMENT 1 below.

### E37 AMENDMENT 1 (declared 2026-08-15 03:35, BEFORE any scored arm)

**Trigger:** a plumbing smoke run against `upstage/solar-pro4` via OpenRouter
(20 questions, $0.0013, declared as path verification and NOT as evidence).
It scored 16/20, and inspecting the four failures showed two of them were the
GRADER's fault, not the model's. X1 and X2 answered a bare `NO` - the correct
judgement, with the conflict genuinely detected - and were scored `wrong`
solely for not restating both figures.

**The amendment.** The `conflict` grader gains a `partial` outcome: a response
that signals disagreement but does not quantify it is `partial`, not `wrong`.
Folding it into `wrong` would score a model that DETECTED the conflict
identically to one that MISSED it, destroying the distinction the category
exists to measure. This is the same principle already applied to
`stale_value` and `format_error`.

**Prediction 2's bands are unchanged** and are resolved on `correct` only -
`partial` does not count as correct. The registered falsifier is unchanged.
**Added to the registered reporting:** `partial` is reported alongside
`correct` for the conflict category, because "detected 3 of 3 conflicts but
quantified 1" is a materially different capability claim from "found 1 of 3"
and the verdict must not blur them.

**Grader re-validated after the change:** `tools/validate_ps_eval.py` now
drives six synthetic respondents - perfect 20/20, stale 4 `stale_value`,
over-claimer 4 `over_claim`, partial 3 `partial`, missed-conflict 3 `wrong`,
prose 20 `format_error`. All six produce their signature.

**Two genuine model failures from the same smoke run, recorded because they
show the instrument works:** solar-pro4 answered C2 as `19.1%` (truncating
19.1748 rather than rounding to one decimal place as asked), and answered C4
as `21.4%` - which is the BOARD MINUTE's figure, when the question explicitly
said "calculated from the financial summary". The second is precisely the
cross-document failure this eval was built to catch.

### E37 RESULT - the two local arms (2026-08-15)

Both arms: llama-server b10435, `-c 16384`, `-np 1`, temperature 0,
`max_tokens 4096`, one request per question, pack resent each time. Relay
down throughout. Full-offload gate asserted and passing on both.

| field | Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M | Qwen3-30B-A3B-2507 (control) |
|---|---|---|
| `correct` /20 | **20** | 14 |
| `citation_valid` /20 | 20 | 18 |
| `over_claim_rate` | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| `stale_values` /4 | 0 | 0 |
| retrieval /5 | 5 | 5 |
| supersession /4 | 4 | 4 |

**Four of the five registered predictions are FALSIFIED, all in the same
direction - I registered bands below what the subject did.**

1. **FALSIFIED.** `over_claim_rate` was registered at **0.50-1.00** for the
   local Q4_K_M and measured **0.00**. The registration went further and
   named the alternative explicitly: "if the local Q4 matches the cloud arm
   at 0.00-0.25, that is the strongest on-prem result available tonight".
   It did, and the cloud arm has not even run yet.
2. **FALSIFIED.** `correct` was registered at **11-16** for Qwen3.8 and
   measured **20**. The workhorse band (9-14) HELD at 14, top of band.
3. **HELD.** `stale_values` 0-2 predicted, 0 measured for both arms.
4. **FALSIFIED for both arms.** The prediction was that `citation_valid`
   would be LOWER than `correct` for every arm, on the reasoning that
   answering and sourcing are different capabilities. Qwen3.8 scored 20 and
   20 - equal, not lower. The workhorse scored citations 18 against correct
   14, i.e. HIGHER, which is the prediction inverted: it frequently cited
   the right clause and then got the answer wrong. That is a more useful
   fact than the one predicted, and it belongs to the control, not the
   subject.
5. **FALSIFIED.** `computation` was registered as the weakest local category
   at 1-3 of 4. Qwen3.8 scored **4/4**. The prediction was right about the
   CONTROL - the workhorse scored 1/4 - and wrong about the model under
   test, which is the distinction that matters.

**The whole-experiment falsifier did NOT fire.** Zero `format_error` on both
arms, so the content scores are interpretable.

**What this measures, stated narrowly.** On a ~2.7K-token pack of six
documents, Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M answered every question in all five categories
correctly, with a valid citation each time and no fabrication on the four
unanswerable items. The control failed the two hardest categories almost
completely (computation 1/4, conflict 0/3) while matching on the three
easier ones, so the instrument discriminates strongly between these two
models - it is not a pass-everything eval.

**But the subject hit the CEILING, and a ceiling cannot rank.** 20/20 leaves
no headroom, so this result establishes a floor for Qwen3.8 and not its
frontier. Nothing about "how capable is this model at knowledge work" is
answered by an instrument it exhausts. E38 exists for that reason.

**Cost, which is part of the verdict and not a footnote.** Qwen3.8 took
597.9s against the control's 16.0s for the same twenty questions - 37.4x the
wall time for +6 correct. Whether that trade is worth taking is a routing
decision, not a benchmark result, and the routing guide has to state it
either way.

**One evidence gap, found by auditing this file rather than by it failing.**
The runner recorded `citation_valid: 20` but did not persist the citation
TEXT, so the number could not be re-checked after the fact. Fixed in
`tools/run_ps_eval.py` (a `citation` field is now written per question); the
E37 figures stand as measured, but they are the last ones in this repo that
cannot be independently audited from the JSON alone.

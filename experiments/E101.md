### Why this experiment exists

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | local coding reliability - parked-trigger 1 territory |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether a sampler setting we have never measured (`min-p`) is a real lever on non-termination, or whether the effect is dominated by `reasoning_effort`, which F71 already measured |
| **decision that changes** | whether production serving gains sampler flags at all. Today it sets none |

### Provenance of the hypothesis

A community report on an RTX 3090 Ti / Windows box describes Qwen3.8-27B quants
looping on long agentic work and proposes `--min-p 0.05` as a candidate fix,
alongside `-temp 1.0 --top-p 0.95 --top-k 20 --presence-penalty 0.0
--repeat-penalty 1.0`. **That is a hypothesis from a different box, a different
OS and a different workload**, and it is tested here as such. Their hardware
constraint (24 GB VRAM) does not apply to this box and none of their quant
selection reasoning transfers.

### The instrument, and the defect that had to be fixed before it could run

`tools/run_coding_eval.py`, task-set `core`, six tasks: `normalize_path` and
`roman_roundtrip` (the two F71 named as RUNAWAY - one produced no code after
51,845 tokens at `xhigh` and working code in 1,469 at `medium`; the other
consumed a full 100,000-token budget without converging), plus
`longest_valid_parens`, `word_break`, `min_window`, `eval_expr` as hard controls
that F71 did not name.

**Both eval harnesses in this repo hardcode `temperature: 0`.** `min_p`, `top_k`
and `top_p` are truncation filters over a sampled distribution; greedy decoding
takes the argmax and never samples, so at temperature 0 they are INERT. Running
the reported config through the unmodified harness would have measured nothing
and produced a clean null. `run_coding_eval.py` gained an opt-in sampler payload
at `d3d02df` and now REFUSES a truncation filter at temperature 0 rather than
report a no-op. Every default is `None` and `None` sends nothing, so the arms
banked before today are unaffected - guarded by
`tests/test_coding_eval_sampler.py`.

`repetition_stats()` records `max_line_run` (longest run of identical adjacent
lines) and `max_ngram_rep` (most occurrences of any 8-word window) per item.
Two measures because a loop takes two shapes and either alone reads clean when
the other is firing. **Counts only. No threshold for "looping" is baked into
the meter; the thresholds are the predictions below.**

### THE POSITIVE CONTROL, RUN BEFORE ANY ARM (F100, F128)

A flag can load without acting, and `min_p` reaching llama-server's OpenAI
endpoint is an ASSUMPTION until exercised. Before any arm runs, on the same
served model:

| check | passes iff |
|---|---|
| **C1 seed determinism** | two requests, `temperature 1.0`, SAME seed -> byte-identical content |
| **C2 seed sensitivity** | two requests, `temperature 1.0`, DIFFERENT seeds -> content differs |
| **C3 min_p acts** | `temperature 1.0, min_p 0.9` vs `min_p 0.0` at the same seed -> content differs |

**If C1-C3 do not all pass, E101 does not run and the failure is the finding.**
C3 is the one that matters: C1 and C2 can both pass while `min_p` is silently
dropped from the payload.

## E101 RESULT - the sampler fix is real and SECOND-ORDER; the effort default is the whole problem. PREDICTION 5 FALSIFIED. (2026-09-04)

Five cells, 78 generations, 402 minutes, quiet GPU (Rule 9 SATISFIED - the relay
window was open throughout, so **these wall times are valid, not void**).
Kernel `7.0.0-29-generic`, build `b10435`, no flash attention (Amendment 1).

| cell | n | pass | truncated | tokens | wall | pass rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c1 greedy, `medium` | 6 | 6 | **0** | 11,194 | 15.4m | 100% |
| c2 sampled min-p 0.00, `medium` | 18 | 18 | **0** | 36,588 | 50.2m | 100% |
| c3 sampled min-p 0.05, `medium` | 18 | 18 | **0** | 42,648 | 58.6m | 100% |
| **c4 min-p 0.00, `xhigh` (THEIR CONFIG)** | 18 | 7 | **11** | **109,547** | 150.5m | **39%** |
| **c5 min-p 0.05, `xhigh` (THEIR FIX)** | 18 | 10 | **8** | 93,270 | 128.2m | **56%** |

### THE ANSWER

> **`min-p` works, and it is worth about a quarter of what the effort setting is
> worth.** Adding `min-p 0.05` at maximum effort removes 3 runaways of 18.
> Dropping the effort from `xhigh` to `medium`, at the same sampler, removes
> **all 11**. The reported fix is real - and it is aimed at the smaller term.

At `medium` effort the failure does not exist to be fixed: **42 of 42
generations completed correctly**, greedy and sampled alike, and `min-p` changed
nothing because there was nothing to change. It cost 17% more tokens (42,648 vs
36,588) for that null.

### Scoring - the six predictions

| # | prediction | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | cell 4 truncated >= 6 of 18 | **HELD** - 11 |
| 2 | cell5 - cell4 <= -3 | **HELD** - exactly -3 (8 vs 11), at the registered boundary |
| 3 | **HEADLINE: effort beats the sampler** | **HELD** - effort -11, min-p -3 |
| 4 | F71 reproduces on the greedy control | **HELD** - both tasks pass |
| 5 | repetition meter tracks non-termination | **FALSIFIED** - 3.2 vs 3.1, ratio 1.03 |
| 6 | transport_failed + no_code = 0 | **HELD** - 0 of 78 |

**P2 held at exactly its registered boundary (-3, threshold <= -3).** A single
run landing on the threshold is the weakest possible pass and is reported as
such: three replicates at n=18 cannot separate "worth 3" from "worth 1" or
"worth 5". The DIRECTION is what this licenses, not the magnitude.

### P5 is the finding, and it falsifies the word "looping"

The registered prediction was that truncated generations would repeat
themselves ~3x more than passing ones. **They do not repeat at all.** Mean
`max_ngram_rep` is 3.2 on truncated items against 3.1 on passing ones - a ratio
of 1.03 - and `max_line_run` is 1 across all 78 generations. In cell 4 the
single highest repetition score in the cell belongs to a generation that
**passed**.

So the failure mode is **non-convergent deliberation, not degenerate
repetition**. The model reasons coherently and never decides to stop. This
matters operationally rather than semantically: `min-p`, `repeat-penalty`,
`presence-penalty` and `top-k` all act on the repetition mechanism, and the
thing consuming the budget here is not that mechanism. It explains both why the
reported fix helps a little (it perturbs sampling enough to reach a stop
sometimes) and why it cannot help much.

### Which tasks run away, and it is not uniform

Truncations by task, cell 4 (3 seeds): `roman_roundtrip` 3/3, `word_break` 3/3,
`eval_expr` 3/3, `normalize_path` 1/3, `min_window` 1/3, `longest_valid_parens`
0/3. Cell 5 removes `normalize_path` and `min_window` entirely and one
`roman_roundtrip`. **Three of six tasks run away every single time at `xhigh`
regardless of sampler**, which is where the residual 8 truncations live.

### Registered decision rule - which branch fired

Registered: *"P2 holds and P3 holds -> min-p is a real but secondary lever.
Recommend it only alongside `medium`, and say so in the same sentence."*

That is the branch. **The actionable line is unchanged from F71 and is not
`min-p`: serve this model with `reasoning_effort: medium` set EXPLICITLY.** The
default is the setting that fails. `min-p 0.05` may be added on top; on this
evidence it is worth roughly a quarter as much and costs tokens where the
failure is already absent.

### What this does NOT establish

- **Nothing about their box.** Different GPU, OS and build. Non-transfer of the
  magnitude is expected; only the mechanism is portable.
- **The `medium` cells cannot distinguish min-p settings at all** - both are at
  ceiling. P2's estimate comes entirely from the `xhigh` cells.
- **One model, one quant, six tasks.** The three always-runaway tasks carry most
  of the effect and a different task set would move the numbers.
- **Nothing about MTP, which is off throughout.**

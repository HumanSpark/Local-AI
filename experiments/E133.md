## E133 - R10 attributed: the RUNTIME breaks it, and q8_0 KV then silences it (2026-09-07)

E132 left `R10` on l5 unattributed with three variables in play. This holds the
runtime constant - **both arms on the new build `daef7b687`** - and varies only
the KV cache precision, at the control's own `-c 16384`, `max_tokens 4096`,
`reasoning_effort: low`.

| config | R01 | R09 | R10 | R12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| old runtime, no KV quant (banked control) | correct | `off_by_calendar` | **correct**, "16 June 2027" | correct |
| **A**: new runtime, `-fa on` | correct | `off_by_calendar` | **`off_by_calendar`, "17 June 2027"** | correct |
| **B**: new runtime, `-fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0` | correct | `off_by_calendar` | **`truncated`, no answer** | correct |

Three of four items are identical in every configuration, including R09's known
failure, which is what makes the fourth interpretable rather than noise.

### Two causes, doing different things

1. **The runtime turns a correct answer into a wrong one.** Arm A matches the
   control's serving flags exactly and still fails, by **one day** - 17 June
   against 16 June 2027. That is [[F89]]/[[F92]]'s boundary shape, not a collapse.
2. **q8_0 KV then turns the wrong answer into no answer.** Arm B stops
   converging and burns the whole 4,096-token budget. The gateway reproduces
   this at `-c 49152` (E132), so it holds across two context sizes.

### [[F110]] needs reading narrowly, and its own text says why

F110 records "q8_0 is free, 4-bit is a 5.6% cliff" **and** that "100 perfect
retrievals measured none of it". A measure at ceiling cannot detect a cost that
only appears where the model is already struggling. **q8_0 is free on items that
converge, and on the one item that no longer converges it is the difference
between a wrong date and silence.**

**An intermediate reading in this session was wrong and is corrected here.** On
arm A alone I said the KV hypothesis was exonerated. Arm B shows it is not - it
is a second, smaller effect stacked on the runtime's.

### What this costs, and what it does not

**One item of 51** across l5, l4 and l3. l4 and l3 reproduce their controls
item-for-item on the new runtime (E132), so this is not general degradation of
document work - it is the single hardest calendar item in the set.

**Rule 9 breached deliberately**, following `run_e76_all.sh`: the gateway was
resident throughout, so **every wall time here is void**. Correctness is not a
contended quantity. The gateway was healthy before and after.

**Limits:** one item, one run per arm, four items per arm. This attributes R10;
it does not measure how often the runtime introduces a one-day boundary error,
which would need the full bank on both builds.

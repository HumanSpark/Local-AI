## E82 PRE-REGISTRATION - bounding the order effect: ten shuffled orders (2026-08-28)

**Registered before any shuffled order runs.** No shuffle has been executed.

### Why this experiment exists

E81 established item order as a live variable in the multi-round tool loop and
bounded it from a **single** alternative order: reverse changed 2 of 24 answers'
text and 0 capability verdicts, deterministically. E81's own limits section said
that is not a sample of orders and that a shuffle study would bound it properly.

**This is that study, and the number it exists to produce is one line:** *by how
much can an arm's total on this bank move, on this instrument, from item order
alone?* Every per-item claim in E79 and E80 inherits the answer.

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | none - instrument work, and it prices the caveat every earlier entry now carries |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether E79 and E80's between-arm comparisons can stand on one order each |
| **decision that changes** | whether the E81 rule (hold order constant) is sufficient, or whether single-order arms have to be re-run across orders before their per-item claims mean anything |

### The design

Ten shuffled orders, seeds **1 to 10**, permutation derived from the seed alone
and recorded in each result file with the resulting `order_ids`, so any run is
reproducible from its own output. Everything else is held: `--router fixed`
replaying `e80-r2-decisions.json`, one server, `-c 16384`, `-fa on`,
`--max-rounds 6`, no interleaved request.

Four runs are already banked and are the reference points, not new arms: bank
order twice (24/24 byte-identical to each other) and reverse order twice (24/24
byte-identical to each other). **The noise floor at fixed order is therefore
measured at zero**, so everything this study sees is order.

### Predictions, each naming the FIELD it is scored against

| # | prediction | scored against |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the total is stable | **spread** (max minus min) of `total correct` across the ten orders **<= 2** |
| 2 | verdicts are much more stable than text | items with **>1 distinct verdict** across the ten orders **<= 3 of 24** |
| 3 | text moves more than verdicts, and more than reverse alone showed | items with **>1 distinct answer text** **>= 3 of 24** |
| 4 | the order-contingent server fault recurs | at least one shuffled order produces a `transport_failed`, as reverse did on `R10` |
| 5 | band E is the stabler band - its items are single-round lookups, not tool loops | items with >1 distinct verdict in band E **<=** the same count in band R |

**Prediction 1 carries the cost.** A spread of 0-1 means E79 and E80's
between-arm gaps - +3 for the tool on band R, -5 on band E, +2 for the best
router - are safely larger than the order noise. A spread of 4 or more would put
E80's entire +2 inside it.

### Registered decision rule, tie-break included

| observed | action |
|---|---|
| spread <= 1 | order is a nuisance and the E81 rule is sufficient. Record the bound and change nothing |
| spread 2-3 | **material.** Every reported total in E79/E80 gains this bound as a stated caveat, and no future claim smaller than the bound may be made from a single order |
| spread >= 4 | **the expensive branch.** Single-order arms cannot support per-item comparisons. E79's conversion and regression lists and E80's router comparison must be re-run across at least three orders before they are quoted again |
| **tie-break:** spread is exactly 2 but driven ENTIRELY by `transport_failed` | that is infrastructure, not order-dependent capability. Report the capability-only spread as the headline and the raw spread beside it - do not let a server fault inflate a capability bound |
| any run fails to produce 24 results | that order is declared and excluded, and the exclusion is reported with the seed |

**Evidence to be written:** `results/raw/e82-shuffle-seed{1..10}-l5.json`,
`results/raw/e82-scores.json`.

## E82 RESULT - the order effect is 2 items, and E80's entire router margin sits inside it. PREDICTION 2 FALSIFIED. (2026-08-28)

Twelve orders of the same 24 items: ten seeded shuffles (seeds 1-10), plus bank
order and reverse order from the banked runs. **All replay
`e80-r2-decisions.json`, so no routing decision varied anywhere.** One server,
Rule 9 breached, wall times VOID.

| total | orders |
|---|---|
| 14/24 | seeds 1, 4, 9 |
| 15/24 | seeds 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, **bank**, **reverse** |
| 16/24 | seed 6 |

### THE NUMBER

> **Item order alone moves this arm's total across a range of 2 items,
> 14 to 16 of 24.** The noise floor at FIXED order is zero - bank order and
> reverse order were each run twice and each pair was 24/24 byte-identical - so
> the whole of this range is order.

**And it is capability, not infrastructure.** The registered tie-break covers a
spread of exactly 2 driven by transport faults; it does not apply. Only `R10`
ever hits a runner-owned outcome, and **it is `wrong` in every run that
completes it**, so a transport fault costs zero correct answers and cannot have
moved the total.

### What sits inside the bound, stated plainly

| claim | size | against a 2-item order bound |
|---|---|---|
| E80: the best router's gain over not routing | **+2** | **entirely inside it** |
| E79: the tool's gain on band R | +3 | just outside |
| E79: the tool's loss on band E | -5 | outside |
| E78: band R separation, workhorse to Qwen3.8 | 8 | far outside |
| E78: band E headroom | 2 | **entirely inside it** |

**E80 recommended nothing on a +2 margin. This says that was right for a reason
E80 could not yet quote: the margin equals the order noise.** The decision rule
there fired on judgement about a small sample; it now has a measurement behind it.

**E78's two-item band-E headroom is also inside the bound** - which does NOT
weaken E78's conclusion, it strengthens it. The claim was that context
engineering has almost nothing to recover; a headroom indistinguishable from
ordering noise is a smaller target, not a larger one.

### Instability, per item

| | count | items |
|---|---|---|
| >1 distinct **verdict** across 12 orders | **4 of 24** | `E05`, `E08` (correct / format_error), `R09` (correct / off_by_calendar), `R10` (wrong / transport_failed) |
| >1 distinct **answer text** | **8 of 24** | `E05`, `E07`, `E08`, `R04`, `R07`, `R08`, `R09`, `R10` |

By band: verdict instability **R=2, E=2**; text instability **R=5, E=3**.

**Every unstable item is one the tool was offered on.** The 9 items where the
replayed decisions withheld the tool did not move at all, in verdict or in text,
across twelve orders. The instability is a property of the multi-round tool loop,
not of the bank.

### Scoring

| # | prediction | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | spread of `total correct` <= 2 | **HELD at the boundary** - exactly 2 |
| 2 | items with >1 distinct verdict <= 3 of 24 | **FALSIFIED** - 4 |
| 3 | items with >1 distinct text >= 3 of 24 | **HELD** - 8, against the 2 that reverse alone revealed |
| 4 | at least one shuffled order produces a `transport_failed` | **HELD** - seeds 3, 5, 7, 8 |
| 5 | band E verdict instability <= band R | **HELD** - equal at 2 each |

**Prediction 3 is the one worth keeping.** Reverse order alone surfaced 2
unstable texts; twelve orders surfaced **8**. A single alternative order
understated the effect fourfold, which is the argument for having run this at
all rather than quoting E81's reverse-only figure.

### The registered rule fires on `spread 2-3`

> *"Material. Every reported total in E79/E80 gains this bound as a stated
> caveat, and no future claim smaller than the bound may be made from a single
> order."*

**Applied:** E79's band-level gains (+3, -5) and E78's band separation (8) stand
clear of it. **E80's +2 does not**, and E80's own conclusion - recommend nothing -
is unchanged and now better supported. No result is withdrawn.

**The standing rule this leaves:** on this instrument, **a difference of 2 items
or fewer, measured from a single item order, is not a result.** Three orders
minimum for any claim that small.

### What this does NOT establish

- **One arm, one bank, one model.** The bound is for the workhorse on L5 with 15
  of 24 items receiving a tool. An arm with no tool offered would likely be
  perfectly stable - the 9 withheld items were - and one with tools everywhere
  would likely be worse.
- **Twelve orders is not a distribution.** The range is 14-16; nothing here
  supports a variance or a confidence interval, and none is quoted.
- **The mechanism is not re-derived.** E81 established prefix displacement and
  order as live variables; this measures the size of the second and does not
  revisit why.

## E116 PRE-REGISTRATION - what does effort COST on a short turn? (2026-09-06)

**Registered before the run.** `results/raw/e116` does not exist.

### Why this experiment exists

The serving proposal's open item 2 says that if the relay mostly serves short
chat turns, setting `--reasoning-effort medium` is *"harmless but not worth
much"*, because E101 found medium and xhigh indistinguishable where nothing ran
away.

**E115b makes that sentence look wrong in the expensive direction.** On a short
reasoning prompt the ladder ran `xhigh` **337** < `low` **542** < `medium`
**633** reasoning characters, and E115's four cells showed the same ordering on
both of its prompts. If that holds across short traffic, switching the default
to `medium` does not leave short turns unchanged - it makes them roughly twice
as expensive. **"Harmless" and "1.9x" are different recommendations**, and the
relay's traffic is mostly short.

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | what the proposed change costs on the traffic the relay actually serves |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether the proposal's "harmless on short turns" clause is true |
| **decision that changes** | whether the recommendation is unconditional, conditional on workload, or withdrawn |

### Design

Nine short turns spread across trivial, factual, light-reasoning and generative
shapes - "short" is not one thing. Three efforts (`xhigh`, `medium`, `low`) per
turn, **one server, effort varied per request**, which E115 established is
honoured and which removes cross-process variation from the comparison. Greedy,
`-np 1`, `--max-tokens 4096`. `tools/e116_short_turn_effort_cost.py`.

**This measures COST, not quality.** Nine short turns cannot rank two efforts on
correctness and no such claim will be made from it; quality on long work is E42's
and E101's question and is already answered.

### Registered decision rule

| observed | action |
|---|---|
| P1 and P2 hold | the proposal is **amended**: the change is a win on long agentic work and a real cost on short turns, so the recommendation becomes conditional on the relay's traffic mix, which nobody has measured |
| P1 fails, ratio in 0.8-1.25 | the proposal's "harmless" clause stands as written and this is recorded as confirming it |

### What this cannot establish

- **Nothing about quality at any effort.** Cost only.
- Nine turns, one model, one build, one prompt phrasing each.
- **Nothing about the relay's real traffic mix**, which remains unmeasured and
  is what would turn this into an actual recommendation. This makes the
  conditional sharp; it does not resolve it.

## E116 RESULT - `medium` costs more on the typical short turn, and the aggregate hides it (2026-09-06)

Nine short turns, three efforts each, one server, effort varied per request,
greedy, `--max-tokens 4096`. No generation hit the cap.

| turn | `xhigh` | `medium` | `low` | medium/xhigh |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| greeting | 102 | 147 | 73 | 1.44 |
| factual | 21 | 105 | 89 | **5.00** |
| definition | 137 | 177 | 137 | 1.29 |
| rewrite | 79 | 133 | 122 | 1.68 |
| extract | 116 | 72 | 70 | 0.62 |
| arith | 58 | 182 | 144 | 3.14 |
| light_reason | 54 | 89 | 99 | 1.65 |
| code_snippet | **484** | 230 | 55 | 0.48 |
| summarise | 82 | 108 | 68 | 1.32 |
| **total** | **1,133** | **1,243** | **857** | **1.10** |
| median | 82 | 133 | 89 | 1.62 |

| # | prediction | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HEADLINE: `medium` total > 1.25x `xhigh` | **FALSIFIED** - 1.097 |
| 2 | `medium` > `xhigh` on >= 7 of 9 turns | held - **7 of 9** |
| 3 | no generation hits the cap | held - 0 of 27 |
| 4 | `low` total between `xhigh` and `medium` | **FALSIFIED** - `low` is BELOW both |

### THE ANSWER, and why the two falsified predictions matter more than the held one

> **On the typical short turn `medium` costs 1.62x the median of `xhigh`, but
> across a realistic mix it costs only 1.10x** - because `xhigh` occasionally
> blows up. Asked for a one-line string reverse, `xhigh` spent **484 tokens and
> 41.6 s** against `medium`'s 230 and 20.4 s.

**The aggregate and the per-turn picture disagree, and reporting either alone
would mislead.** One outlier carries the total: remove `code_snippet` and the
ratio moves from 1.10 to 1.30. So the honest statement is two numbers, not one.

**This reframes the proposal's benefit.** My own proposal leads with *"three
times the tokens for 39% of the completions"*, which is true of long agentic
work and not of short traffic. What `medium` actually buys on short turns is
**variance reduction, not mean cost reduction** - it is mildly more expensive
most of the time and much cheaper when `xhigh` fails to converge. On long work
that same tail is catastrophic (E101: 11 of 18 hitting the cap), which is why
the change is worth making at all.

**`low` is cheapest everywhere on this bank** (0.756x `xhigh`) and is NOT
recommended on that basis: E42 measured `low` at 5/8 against `medium`'s 7/8 on
the expert tier. Cheap and worse is a different trade from cheap and equal.

### What this does NOT establish

- **Cost only, never quality.** Nine short turns cannot rank efforts on
  correctness and no such claim is made here.
- **The relay's traffic mix is still unmeasured**, and it is what converts this
  into a recommendation. This sharpens the conditional; it does not resolve it.

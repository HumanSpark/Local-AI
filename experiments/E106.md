## E106 PRE-REGISTRATION - is temperature 0 hamstringing the models, and is it hamstringing them UNEQUALLY? (2026-09-05)

**Registered before any sampled expert-tier run exists.** `results/raw/e106`
does not exist.

### Why this experiment exists

**Every capability figure in this repo is measured at `temperature: 0`.** Both
GGUFs benched tonight ship `general.sampling.temp = 1.0`, `top_k = 20`,
`top_p = 0.95` in their headers as the vendor's recommendation, and every eval
here overrides all three. That choice has never been tested against a bank that
can detect the difference.

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | the METHOD underneath both headline questions |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether greedy decoding understates capability, and whether it understates DIFFERENT models by different amounts |
| **decision that changes** | if the understatement is unequal, **E104's 7/4/1 ordering is partly an artefact of our harness rather than the weights**, and the quant recommendation is re-opened |

### The one existing measurement, and why it cannot answer

E101 c1 vs c2, same six tasks, same effort, only sampling changed:

| config | pass | tokens per 6-task run |
|---|---|---|
| greedy, temp 0 | 6/6 | 11,194 |
| shipped defaults, 3 seeds | 18/18 | 12,196 |

**No difference, and the bank was saturated** - every generation in both arms
passed. Per F145 that licenses "no difference detected", never "temperature 0 is
fine". This experiment moves the same comparison onto the bank that separated
three models by six points.

### Why temp 0 might understate, stated as a mechanism rather than a worry

Greedy takes the argmax at every step and cannot leave a locally-attractive,
globally-wrong path. **E101 measured that escape happening**: at `xhigh`, adding
`min-p 0.05` recovered 3 runaways of 18 (F140), because perturbing the
distribution let stuck generations reach a stop token. That is termination
rather than accuracy, but it is the same mechanism, and it is evidence from this
box rather than from the literature.

**What temp 0 buys, and why it is not simply abandoned:** byte-determinism
(F59, F88, F96) makes a single run a real measurement. Sampled arms need seeds -
E101 spent 402 minutes on six tasks partly for that reason.

### Design - two stages, the second registered NOW and conditional

**Stage 1.** `Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M`, expert tier, `-c 32768 --max-tokens 24576
--thinking medium`, at the SHIPPED defaults (`--temp 1.0 --top-p 0.95 --top-k 20
--min-p 0.0`), **3 seeds** (201, 202, 203). Comparator: **E104's banked 7/8**,
same session, build, kernel and config, differing only in sampling.

**Stage 2, conditional and registered in advance:** if stage 1's mean exceeds
7/8, the SAME sampled config runs on `GSQ-RCO-IQ3_S` for 3 seeds, against its
banked 4/8. **This is the stage that matters** - a uniform understatement
cancels in a comparison; an unequal one does not.

Registering stage 2 now is the point: deciding to run it after seeing an
interesting stage 1 would be choosing the follow-up that suits the result.

### Predictions, each naming the FIELD it is scored against

| # | prediction | scored against |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HEADLINE: greedy does not understate** | mean `pass` over the 3 sampled seeds **<= 7.0** |
| 2 | sampling does not COST accuracy either | min `pass` across the 3 seeds **>= 6** |
| 3 | sampling costs tokens, as it did at `medium` on the easy bank | mean total `completion_tokens` **>** E104 incumbent's 74,796 |
| 4 | `sliding_median` is a capability limit, not a decoding artefact | it fails in **at least 2 of 3** seeds |
| 5 | seeds actually differ - the arm is not accidentally deterministic | the 3 seeds do NOT all produce byte-identical output on every task |
| 6 | the harness holds | `transport_failed` + `no_code` = 0 |

**P5 exists because a sampled arm that silently ran greedy would satisfy P1
perfectly.** E101's C1/C2/C3 control proved the sampler acts on this build, but
that was a different model and a different invocation path, and an equality that
could not have failed is what caught two degenerate controls in this session.

### Registered decision rule

| observed | action |
|---|---|
| P1 holds (mean <= 7.0) | **temperature 0 does not understate on this bank.** Every figure in this repo stands as measured. Record it and stop; stage 2 does NOT run |
| **P1 FALSIFIED (mean > 7.0)** | greedy understates the incumbent. **Run stage 2 immediately** - and until stage 2 reports, E104's 7/4/1 ordering is provisional and no quant recommendation may be restated |
| P1 falsified AND stage 2 shows the quant gains MORE | **the harness biased E104.** F144 is amended, the quant recommendation is withdrawn pending a full sampled re-run of all three arms |
| P1 falsified AND stage 2 shows the quant gains the same or less | the understatement is uniform or favours us; the E104 ordering survives and is restated with the sampled figures beside it |
| P5 fails | the arm did not sample. Nothing may be concluded; fix the invocation and re-run |

### What this cannot establish

- **3 seeds on 8 items.** This can detect a large, consistent understatement. It
  cannot resolve a one-item difference, and P1's falsification branch demands
  stage 2 rather than a conclusion.
- **One sampler point.** The vendor default is tested, not a sweep; a different
  temperature could behave differently.
- **Nothing about `pass@k`.** Every arm is scored on a single generation per
  task, which is the deployment shape this repo cares about.

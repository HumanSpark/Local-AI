## E84 PRE-REGISTRATION - KA-H2: the same questions up the whole context ladder, and the instrument defect that made that impossible (2026-08-28)

**Registered before any arm runs.** The preflight below is measurement, not an
arm.

### The instrument defect, found while preparing this run

`spikes/kw-eval/kw_corpus.py` states the ladder's design intent in its own
header: *"THE THREE SIZES ARE THE SAME MATERIAL, GROWING. A question answerable
in S must stay answerable in L, which is what makes the ladder a measurement of
context handling rather than of three different tests."*

**It was never run that way.** The bank tags each item with the pack it is
*introduced* in, and `tools/run_kw_eval.py` filtered `i["pack"] == args.pack`.
So every banked KA-H1 arm asked only the questions its own pack added:

| pack | items asked | qwen38 banked |
|---|---|---|
| s | 11 (s-only) | **8/11** |
| m | 13 (m-only) | **13/13** |
| l | **2** (l-only) | never run |

8/11 and 13/13 are **not a degradation curve** - they are two different tests.
And `--pack l`, run as designed, would have asked **two questions**, which after
E82's standing rule (a difference of 2 items or fewer from one configuration is
not a result) could not have produced a result at all.

**Fix, and its default:** `--item-packs` separates *which questions* from *which
context*. Default is `{args.pack}` - byte-identical to the pre-2026-08-28
behaviour, verified: s→11, m→13, l→2. Items may be asked against their own pack
or any larger one, and never a smaller one, because packs are prefixes of one
ordered document list so a smaller context is missing the evidence. The chosen
set is recorded in each result's `meta.item_packs`.

tree that now gives 56,223 and 144,428.

Token counts from the **server's own tokenizer**, never chars/4:

Workhorse at `-c 131072` measured **32.47 GiB** GTT - the >60 GiB regime is not

### Arms - qwen38, the only local arm with banked s AND m

Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M (15.66 GiB), F84's graduated operating point
(`--spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 4`) as the banked runs used, greedy
(`temperature: 0`), `max_tokens` 1024.

| arm | context pack | items asked | `-c` | what it is for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | s | s (11) | 32768 | **control** - must reproduce banked 8/11 |
| **B** | m | s,m (24) | 65536 | ladder rung 2; its m-subset re-controls banked 13/13 |
| **C** | l | s,m,l (26) | 131072 | **the l pack, first ever run** |
| **D** | s | s (11) | 131072 | **isolator** - big KV allocation, small pack |

The ladder is read on the **11 s-items only**, which are the sole questions asked
identically in all four arms. D exists because A, B and C co-vary pack size with
`-c`; D moves `-c` alone, so a drop in C can be attributed to context *content*
rather than to allocating a 131K KV cache.

### Predictions, each naming the FIELD it is scored against

| # | prediction | scored against | falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | the control reproduces, bridging kernel `7.0.0-28`→`7.0.0-29`, the pinned corpus and the patched runner at once | arm A `correct` | anything other than **8 of 11** |
| 2 | the s-items do NOT degrade up the ladder | spread of s-subset `correct` across **A, B, C** ≤ **1** | spread ≥ 2 |
| 3 | KV allocation alone changes nothing | arm D `correct` equals arm A `correct`, **and** ≥ 9 of 11 answers byte-identical to A | `correct` differs |
| 4 | the m-items re-control | arm B m-subset `correct` | anything other than **13 of 13** |
| 5 | nothing is runner-owned | `outcome_counts` on every arm | any `transport_failed` or `truncated` |

**Prediction 2 is the substantive one and it is registered against my own prior.**
The programme's working expectation has been that the `l` pack is where a
retrieval or context-engineering intervention would finally have something to
recover. Predicting *no* degradation is predicting that KA-H2 closes negative and
that hypothesis loses its last untested home.

### Decision rule, fixed in advance

- **Spread ≤ 1 across A/B/C** - long context does not degrade answerable
  questions on this corpus at 70,415 tokens. **KA-H2 closes negative**, the `l`
  pack is retired as an instrument, and context engineering has no measured
  headroom left to recover on this bank.
- **Spread ≥ 2, and D matches A** - the degradation is context *content*. That is
  a real target: register the retrieval intervention against the s-items at pack
  l, where the loss is now located and quantified.
- **Spread ≥ 2, and D also drops** - it is the KV allocation, not the pack. That
  is a serving finding, not a knowledge-work one, and belongs with E77.
- **Arm A does not reproduce 8/11** - stop. The bridge, the pin or the patch is
  broken and nothing else in the run may be read until that is resolved.
- **Tie-break:** 11 items is a coarse ruler. A 1-item move is inside noise and is
  reported as no change; only ≥2 counts. No confidence interval is quoted from
  11 items.


## E84 RESULT - KA-H2: 70,415 tokens rewrites every answer and changes no verdict. PREDICTIONS 3, 4 AND 5 FALSIFIED. (2026-08-28)

**Runs:** `results/raw/e84-{a,b,c,d,e}-qwen38.json`, scored by
`tools/score_e84.py` into `results/raw/e84-scores.json`. qwen38, corpus pinned
at `98dad04`, kernel `7.0.0-29-generic`, greedy, MTP operating point. GPU peak
28.71 GiB - the >60 GiB regime was never entered.

### Declared amendment: arm E was NOT pre-registered

Arms A-D were registered before any ran. **Arm E was added after arm D returned**,
because D falsified prediction 3 and in doing so invalidated the ladder A/B/C was
built on. Arm E is therefore reported as a **measurement, not a scored
prediction** - no prediction attaches to it, and the registered decision rule is
applied to A/B/C as written before it is discussed.

### What arm D found, which is the most useful result here

Arm D changed **one thing** against arm A: `-c` from 32,768 to 131,072. Same
pack, same 11 questions, same weights, greedy decoding.

| | A | D |
|---|---|---|
| s-items correct | 8/11 | **9/11** |
| verdicts identical to A | - | 10/11 |
| answers byte-identical to A | - | **4/11** |

**Allocating a larger KV cache rewrote 7 of 11 answers and moved a verdict.**
Nothing about the model, the prompt or the questions changed. That is a serving
finding, and it means **arms A, B and C cannot speak to context length at all** -
each of them moved pack content and `-c` together, so any difference between them
is unattributable. The registered ladder was confounded, and its own isolator is
what proved it.

### The clean ladder - `-c` held at 131,072, only pack CONTENT varies

| arm | pack | prompt tokens | s-subset correct | answers rewritten vs D |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D | s | 14,970 | **9/11** | - |
| E | m | 38,329 | **9/11** | 10/11 |
| **C** | **l** | **71,739** | **9/11** | **11/11** |

**Spread 0.** Going from 14,970 to 71,739 tokens - a 4.8x increase, the largest
context this lab has put real questions through - changes **not one verdict**,
while rewriting **every single answer**.

That pairing is the finding. Verdict counts alone would have reported "nothing
happened"; the text says the model was comprehensively perturbed and arrived at
the same answers anyway. It is the E77 pattern at a different lever: there,
`q8_0` KV quantisation rewrote 9 answers in 10 and moved no verdict.

### Scoring

| # | prediction | scored against | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | control reproduces the bank | arm A `correct` | **HELD** - 8/11, and 11/11 verdicts identical to `kw-qwen38-s.json` |
| 2 | s-items do not degrade | spread across A/B/C | **HELD** - spread 1 (but see below: A/B/C is confounded, so this held for a reason the prediction did not name) |
| 3 | KV allocation alone changes nothing | arm D vs A | **FALSIFIED** - 9 vs 8 correct, 4/11 answers identical |
| 4 | arm B's m-subset re-controls 13/13 | arm B m-subset | **FALSIFIED** - 12/13 |
| 5 | nothing runner-owned | `outcome_counts` | **FALSIFIED as worded** - see below |

**Prediction 1 is the load-bearing one** and it bridges three things at once:
kernel `7.0.0-28`→`7.0.0-29`, the pinned corpus against a drifted working tree,
and the patched runner. Arm A's `measured_prompt_tokens` of **14,970** matches
the banked file exactly, as does arm B's **38,329**.

**Prediction 2 held, but not for the reason it was written.** It predicted no
degradation up a ladder that turned out to co-vary two things. The clean ladder
reaches the same conclusion properly, and that is what the finding rests on -
not this.

**Prediction 4's falsification is probably the instrument, not the model.** Arm B
asks the 13 m-items inside a 24-item run; the banked file asked them in a
13-item run. E81 and E82 established that in a shared llama-server slot, item
order and preceding traffic change answers deterministically. One m-item moving
under a different preceding sequence is exactly that effect, and E82 bounded it
at 2 items on a 24-item bank. **No capability claim is made from this.**

**Prediction 5 was badly worded by me and its falsification is mine, not the
runner's.** `transport_failed` is **0 on all five arms** - nothing runner-owned
occurred. What the check actually counted was `hit_token_cap`: answers reaching
the 1,024-token generation cap. That is model verbosity against a configured
limit, it is **present in the banked baseline at the same rate** (arm A 2 of 11,
`kw-qwen38-s.json` 2 of 11), and folding it into a runner-fault check was a
category error in the prediction. The two must not share a field.

### The registered decision rule fires

> *"Spread ≤ 1 across A/B/C - long context does not degrade answerable questions
> on this corpus at 70,415 tokens. KA-H2 closes negative, the `l` pack is retired
> as an instrument, and context engineering has no measured headroom left to
> recover on this bank."*

**It fires, and the clean ladder independently agrees at spread 0.** But the
conclusion is stated with the resolution it actually has:

**KA-H2 closes: no capability degradation from context content up to 71,739
tokens, on this corpus, on one model, at a resolution of 11 items.**

### The instrument defect this experiment exists because of

The banked KA-H1 ladder was **never a ladder**. `run_kw_eval.py` filtered items
to `i["pack"] == args.pack`, so each size was asked only the questions it
introduced: s got 11, m got 13, and `--pack l` would have asked **2**. qwen38's
banked 8/11 at s and 13/13 at m are two different tests. `--item-packs` separates
which questions from which context; the default is byte-identical to the old
behaviour (verified s→11, m→13, l→2).

### What this does NOT establish

- **One model.** qwen38 only. The workhorse - the production model - has no
  ladder, and generalisation is not claimed.
- **The ruler is coarse.** 11 items resolve to 9 percentage points each. A flat
  ladder bounds the loss at *under roughly 2 of 11*; it does not show it is zero,
  and **it does not license retiring H1-H5**. No confidence interval is quoted
  from 11 items.
- **Retrieval was not tested.** This measures whether long context *hurts*, not
  whether retrieval *helps*. Those are different experiments.
- **gpt-oss-120b was not run and will not be** - see below.
- **The `-c` effect is unexplained.** D proves allocation changes output; nothing
  here says why. It belongs with E77's serving work, not with knowledge work.

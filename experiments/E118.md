**I ran E118 without pre-registering it.** The script was written, chained and
executed; no predictions and no decision rule were recorded beforehand, and
CLAUDE.md's rule is that predictions go in the results file BEFORE runs.

**The data is not discarded and it is not promoted.** It is reported below as an
UNREGISTERED OBSERVATION, and no prediction is scored against it, because
predictions written after seeing results are not predictions. Where it agrees

required first.

pre-registration step was skipped in the rush to keep the GPU fed after the
six-hour idle. **The queue discipline that fixed the idle GPU created the
conditions for this.** E119 and E120 were pre-registered; E118 was not.

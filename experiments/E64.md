## E64: what does gpt-oss-120b cost on the REAL traffic mix? (2026-08-19, pre-registered before any run)

**Prerequisite 3, the efficiency question.** E62 established capability (12/12,
reproduced bit-for-bit) and E63 established robustness (shallow 9/10 -> deep
9/10, no degradation). Neither says what it costs to answer, and F86's ordering
is explicit: capability -> robustness -> efficiency -> system value. A model that
passes the first two and takes 30 seconds on a chat turn has no route.

**The instrument is E58's, unchanged**, over the three tiers built from 952 real
relay requests: `chat` (91.3% of traffic, median 68 prompt tokens),
`document` (the 3.9% between), `folder` (the 1.4% carrying 34% of generation
time).

**Comparability, stated rather than assumed.** gpt-oss-120b runs ALONE as a new
`--arm-set gptoss`, and is compared against the **banked** E58 residency figures
rather than re-running them. Same day, same kernel (`7.0.0-28-generic`), same
server binary (`llama.cpp/wt/b10435`), same `--server-extra -b 512 -ub 128`,
same `--ctx 32768`, same tier definitions. Re-running the other four would spend
~15 minutes of GPU reproducing figures already pinned and would create a second
measurement of them to reconcile. **The one condition that differs from E62/E63
is `-b 512 -ub 128`**, which those runs did not use - so these latency numbers
are not directly comparable to E62's 178.0s wall, and that is by design: this
arm must match E58, not E62.

### The numbers it is measured against (E58, warm medians)

| arm | chat | document | folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| workhorse 30B-A3B | **0.91s** | 0.38s | 0.29s |
| Qwen3.8-27B `off` | 5.48s | 3.20s | 1.77s |
| Qwen3.8-27B `low` | 22.15s | 16.54s | 50.10s |
| Qwen3-8B | 9.46s | 8.61s | 51.29s |

### Predictions

1. **PRIMARY: chat warm median between 3s and 8s** - slower than the workhorse
   by a wide margin, in the same band as the 27B at `off`. E61 put it at 2.7x
   the dense 27B's wall on L3-hard, but that tier rewards its lower token count;
   chat is where E58 showed token COUNT dominating token RATE.
2. **It does not beat 0.91s on chat**, so it does not displace the workhorse as
   the resident model. The two-model architecture stands.
3. **Median generated tokens on chat between 50 and 250** - it emitted 392
   median on L3-hard, well below GLM's 1,640, and E58's lesson is that a model
   that will not stop is slow regardless of its rate.
4. **Cold load over 100s.** 59.03 GiB off disk; the 27B's folder cold was
   100.71s at a quarter the weight.

**Falsifiers, and what each means:**

- **Chat warm median under 2.0s** - it becomes a candidate RESIDENT model, not
  just a reviewer, and the architecture question reopens for the first time
  since E60 closed it. This is the result that would most change the programme.
- **Chat warm median over 15s** - it is unusable on 91.3% of traffic and its
  role narrows to the offline folder tier only, whatever its capability.
- **A decode hang on this load** - instrument failure, not a latency figure
  (the E61 rule stands), and the denominator becomes 1 in 8.

**Cost.** One arm, three tiers, one 59.03 GiB load. Near-edge for the amdgpu
suballocator deadlock: box quiet, run attended.

### E64 RESULT: 3.66s on chat, and 4-7.7x FASTER than the reasoning arms on real work (2026-08-19 08:17)

| tier | gpt-oss-120b warm | median gen tokens |
|---|---|---|
| chat | **3.66s** | 185 |
| document | **4.14s** | 202 |
| folder | **6.68s** | 260 |

Cold: 4.73s / 17.67s / 117.68s. Model load 22.0s. Full-offload gate passed, 74
layers, all `Vulkan0`. No hang.

**Against the banked E58 arms, and the split is the whole finding.** Two groups,
and reading the table without separating them gives the wrong answer:

**Arms that emit a comparable amount of text** - gpt-oss-120b is much faster:

| | chat | document | folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.8-27B `low` | **6.05x slower** | **4.00x slower** | **7.50x slower** |
| Qwen3-8B | **2.58x slower** | **2.08x slower** | **7.68x slower** |

**Arms that emit almost nothing** - gpt-oss-120b is slower, and the comparison is
close to meaningless:

| | chat | document | folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| workhorse 30B-A3B | 0.91s (gen 82) | 0.38s (gen 28) | 0.29s (gen **11**) |
| Qwen3.8-27B `off` | 5.48s (gen 57) | 3.20s (gen 28) | 1.77s (gen **11**) |

**Eleven generated tokens is not an answer to a folder question.** On the tier
carrying 34% of all real generation time, the workhorse and the 27B at `off`
return eleven tokens while gpt-oss-120b returns 260. E58's lesson applied in the
opposite direction: there it was a model looking fast on tok/s and being slow to
a user, here it is models looking fast on wall time by not answering. **Compare
latency only within a band of comparable output volume**, or the number measures
brevity rather than speed.

**The routing answer, and the architecture is unchanged.** gpt-oss-120b does not
displace the workhorse: 3.66s against 0.91s on the 91.3% of traffic that is
chat, so the resident model stays. What it does is beat **every reasoning-capable
arm on every tier**, including 7.50x against the 27B at `low` on the folder tier,
which is precisely the difficult 5% the two-model architecture exists to serve.

**Predictions, scored:**

1. chat warm median 3-8s - **HELD** at 3.66s
2. does not beat 0.91s on chat - **HELD**
3. chat median generated tokens 50-250 - **HELD** at 185
4. "cold load over 100s" - **AMBIGUOUSLY WORDED, and that is a defect in the
   pre-registration rather than a result.** It named the 27B's *folder cold* of
   100.71s as its comparator, and on that reading it held: folder cold is
   117.68s. On the reading "time to load the model" it is falsified: load was
   **22.0s**. Both figures are reported and neither is claimed as a hit.
5. Neither falsifier fired (chat is neither under 2.0s nor over 15s), so the
   architecture question does not reopen and the model is not confined to
   offline work.

**A confound worth recording for anyone comparing load times.** This load took
22.0s where E62's took roughly two minutes for the same 59.03 GiB. The page
cache was warm from E62 and E63 earlier in the session. Model load time on this
box is a statement about the page cache as much as about the model, and
`run_step8_leg.sh`'s missing stack fingerprint does not capture it either.

**Hang denominator: one in eight loads** on 2026-08-19. Seven consecutive clean.

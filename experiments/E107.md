## E107 PRE-REGISTRATION - does temperature increase FABRICATION on evidence work? (2026-09-05)

**Registered before any sampled L5 run exists.** `results/raw/e107` does not exist.

### Why this experiment exists

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | production serving, and the client-facing question directly - "should you turn the temperature up on your document assistant?" |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether sampling degrades evidence work, and specifically whether it increases confident fabrication |
| **decision that changes** | whether `temperature 0` stays the right serving default for knowledge work, and whether the routing guide needs a sampler line |

`tools/run_ps_eval.py` states it in its own header: **the headline metric is
`over_claim_rate`, not accuracy** - in professional services a confident
fabrication is a liability event while "not in the pack" is a correct and
billable answer. L5 carries **4 deliberately underspecified items** that exist
to catch exactly this, and no one has ever varied the sampler against them.

### Saturation runs the RIGHT way for this question

F142 retired L5 for model comparison: three models, 23/24, zero separation. That
is a ceiling problem, and **this experiment does not need headroom above - it
needs room below.** The question is not "does temperature help" but **"does
temperature break what greedy already gets right?"** There are 23 points of room
in that direction.

**Evidence the bank can actually see a perturbation this small:** E85 measured
q8_0 KV changing **2 of 24 verdicts** on the production build. So it responds.
E86 then showed that difference did not survive a 1% corpus change, so it
responds NOISILY.

### The detection floor, stated before any result

**E82 measured prompt ORDER alone moving 2 items** across twelve shuffles, and
named `E05`, `E08`, `R09` and `R10` as the unstable ones. **Any effect smaller
than ~2 items is inside noise this repo has already characterised.** That is why
each sampled cell gets 3 seeds and why a 1-item difference will not be reported
as a finding.

### Design

`--tier l5`, 24 items, `-c 16384`, `--server-extra '-fa on'`, **`--thinking low`**
(matched to E78 and E102 so the temp-0 cell is comparable to a banked 23/24),
build `b10435`, quiet GPU.

| cell | temperature | top-p / top-k | seeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | **0** (greedy) | none - inert at temp 0 | 1 (byte-deterministic, F88/F96) |
| T04 | **0.4** | 0.95 / 20 | 3 (301, 302, 303) |
| T07 | **0.7** | 0.95 / 20 | 3 |
| T10 | **1.0** - the models' OWN shipped default | 0.95 / 20 | 3 |

10 runs at ~22 min each, ~4 hours. `top_p`/`top_k` held at the values both GGUFs
ship so the sweep moves ONE axis. Multivariate is deliberately NOT folded in.

### Predictions, each naming the FIELD it is scored against

| # | prediction | scored against |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HEADLINE: temperature increases fabrication** | mean `over_claim` at T10 minus `over_claim` at T0 **>= 1** of 4 |
| 2 | **DISCRIMINATING: evidence work degrades with temperature** | mean `band E correct` at T10 **<** T0's, by **>= 2** |
| 3 | reasoning work is less affected than evidence work | (T0 - T10) on `band R` **<** (T0 - T10) on `band E` |
| 4 | 0.4 is safe - the low-temp intuition holds | mean `total correct` at T04 **>= 22** of 24 (T0 is 23) |
| 5 | the arm actually sampled | across the 3 seeds of T10, at least one item has **>1 distinct answer string** |
| 6 | the graders hold | `format_error` **= 0** across all 240 items |

**P5 is not a formality.** A cell that silently ran greedy would satisfy P1-P4
by producing no change at all, and reporting "temperature is harmless" would be
the exact false negative this session has already produced twice from saturated
equality tests.

### Registered decision rule

| observed | action |
|---|---|
| P1 holds | **temperature increases fabrication on evidence work.** That is the finding, it is client-facing, and `temperature 0` is confirmed as the serving default for document work |
| P1 and P2 both fail, all cells within 1 of T0 | **sampler settings are irrelevant for evidence work at the effort we serve.** A real result that retires a class of tuning speculation - and it is reported with the E82 noise floor beside it, because "no effect" and "an effect smaller than our floor" are not the same claim |
| P4 fails (0.4 already costs >= 2) | the low-temperature intuition does not hold here. Worth a bisect between 0 and 0.4 |
| monotonic degradation across 0 -> 0.4 -> 0.7 -> 1.0 | strongest possible form of P2; a dose-response beats any single contrast and licenses a recommendation without a re-run |
| P5 fails | the cell did not sample. Nothing may be concluded; fix and re-run |

### What this cannot establish

- **One model.** Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M only. Nothing about the quant or ThinkingCap.
- **One axis.** `top_p`/`top_k` are held, not swept. A multivariate screen is
  deliberately deferred until temperature is known to move something.
- **24 items, 3 seeds.** Detects an effect of ~2 items or larger; smaller is
  inside E82's order noise and will be reported as undetected, not absent.
- **Nothing about coding**, where E101 already measured the sampler at `medium`
  and found nothing on a saturated bank.


## E107 RESULT - temperature does NOT cause fabrication; the entire cost is ~1 calendar item. PREDICTIONS 1, 2 AND 3 FALSIFIED. (2026-09-05)

Ten runs, 240 graded items, `--tier l5 -c 16384 --server-extra '-fa on'
--thinking low`, build `b10435`, kernel `7.0.0-29-generic`, quiet GPU
throughout.

| temp | runs | correct | band R | band E | **over_claim** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0.0 greedy** | 1 | 23.00 | 11.00 | 12.00 | **0** |
| 0.4 | 3 | 22.00 [22,22,22] | 10.00 | 12.00 | **0** |
| 0.7 | 3 | 22.33 [23,22,22] | 10.33 | 12.00 | **0** |
| **1.0 (shipped default)** | 3 | 21.67 [21,22,22] | 10.00 | 11.67 | **0** |

### THE ANSWER

> **Raising the temperature does not make this model invent things.** Zero
> over-claims in all ten runs at every temperature - 40 opportunities across the
> four deliberately underspecified items, declined every time. The primary
> hypothesis is dead, and the client-facing answer is the opposite of what was
> registered.
>
> **The whole measured cost is about one item, and it is calendars.** 15 of the
> 17 failures across nine sampled runs are `off_by_calendar`.

### The control reproduced for the THIRD time

`e107-t0` scored 23/24, band R 11/12, band E 12/12, single miss `R09` - matching
E78 (2026-08-28) and E102 (2026-09-04) exactly. Its `sampler` field is `null`,
confirming the harness change made hours earlier still sends the historical
greedy payload. That was the thing most likely to break silently.

### Scoring

| # | prediction | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HEADLINE: temperature increases fabrication** | **FALSIFIED** - delta 0.00 of 4 |
| 2 | evidence work degrades (band E drop >= 2) | **FALSIFIED** - 0.33, inside the noise floor |
| 3 | reasoning less affected than evidence | **FALSIFIED, and INVERTED** - band R -1.00 vs band E -0.33 |
| 4 | 0.4 is safe (>= 22 of 24) | **HELD** - 22.00, all three seeds exactly 22 |
| 5 | the arm actually sampled | **HELD** - 8 of 24 items vary across the t10 seeds |
| 6 | `format_error` = 0 across 240 | **HELD** - 0 |

### P3 is falsified in the direction worth having

The registration predicted reasoning would be LESS affected than evidence. It is
**more** affected. Band E holds 12/12 in eight of nine sampled runs; band R
loses an item in every sampled cell.

**The mechanism this suggests:** temperature perturbs a multi-step deterministic
CALCULATION, where one wrong token propagates and cannot be recovered, and
leaves judgements that are recoverable from context alone - which of four
instruments governs, whether an agreed record is contradicted, whether the pack
is silent. That aligns with F80 (walking a calendar is the weakest measured
capability), F92 (two different models miscounted the same deadline the same
way) and F75 (order changes the wording, not the advice).

### Every contrast is INSIDE the registered noise floor, and that is stated first

E82 measured prompt order alone moving 2 items. **The full 0.0 -> 1.0 drift is
1.33 items.** The honest claim is **"no effect detected above characterised
noise"**, not "temperature is harmless" - and the floor was registered before
the runs precisely so it could not be argued past afterwards.

**There is also no dose-response.** 0.7 (22.33) scored ABOVE 0.4 (22.00), and one
0.7 seed matched greedy exactly. Whatever the cost is, it appears on leaving
greedy and does not grow with temperature over this range.

### A pattern that is NOT a finding, recorded so it can be tested

`R10` returned `truncated` at 0.7 (1 of 3 seeds) and again at 1.0 (1 of 3),
absent from greedy and from 0.4. Two instances in six runs, same item both
times. **Non-termination is a different failure class from a wrong answer**, and
F141 established that this model's runaway mode is non-convergent deliberation.
Two instances cannot carry a claim; it earns its own experiment, not a sentence
in this one.

### Operational line

**Keep `temperature 0` for document work, but the margin is one calendar item,
not a cliff.** For a workload with no date arithmetic in it, sampler choice
looks close to irrelevant on this bank. `0.4` costs exactly one item in all
three seeds and fabricates nothing.

### What this cannot establish

- **One model** (`Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M`), one effort setting (`low`), one bank.
- **One axis.** `top_p` 0.95 / `top_k` 20 held throughout at the shipped values.
- **Detects ~2 items or larger.** Everything measured is smaller than that, so
  every contrast above is reported as undetected rather than absent.
- **Nothing about pass@k.** One generation per item, which is the deployment
  shape.

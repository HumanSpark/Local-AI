## E124 PRE-REGISTRATION - Nemotron on the core bank, the workhorse's actual job (2026-09-07)

**Registered before the run.** `results/raw/e124` does not exist.

### Why this experiment exists

E121 and E122 give the workhorse-swap case its accuracy and cost sides on
document work: Nemotron is more than twice as accurate on l4 and l3, 32% slower
at `tg128`, and 5.68 GiB larger. **But l3 and l4 are contract-document work, and
that is not what the workhorse spends its day doing.** It is the always-loaded
general model.

The core coding bank is the cheapest available proxy for general instruct work
with a banked control: **`e33-workhorse` scored 12/15**. Nemotron has never been
run on it. Without that number the swap case rests entirely on a task shape the
slot was not chosen for.

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | whether the candidate holds up on the incumbent's ordinary work |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether the l3/l4 advantage generalises or is document-specific |
| **decision that changes** | whether a workhorse swap proposal is written or dropped |

### Design

`nvidia_Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B-Q4_K_M`, `--task-set core` (15 tasks), build
`b10435`, `-c 32768 --max-tokens 24576`, `--exec-timeout 15`, quiet GPU.

**Effort is NOT set and CANNOT be**: E123 measured `reasoning_effort` as INERT
for this model - every level including a nonsense one gives byte-identical
output. `--thinking default` is therefore the only honest setting and no
matched-effort claim is available.

**Control:** `e33-workhorse`, **12/15**, same bank. Note the control ran on an
older build; the bank and the scoring are unchanged, and the comparison is
between models rather than between builds.

### Predictions, each naming the FIELD it is scored against

| # | prediction | scored against |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HEADLINE: Nemotron is not worse than the workhorse on general work** | `passed` **>= 12** of 15 |
| 2 | it does not reach the incumbent smart model's ceiling | `passed` **<= 14**, against `e102b-incumbent-q4km`'s 15/15 |
| 3 | the non-termination seen on l4's N2 does not recur here | `truncated` = **0** |
| 4 | the harness holds | `no_code` and `transport_failed` both **0** |

**P1 is the swap gate.** Below 12 the l3/l4 advantage is document-specific and
the swap case dies, however good the calendar numbers are.

### Registered decision rule

| observed | action |
|---|---|
| `passed` >= 12 | the advantage generalises. **Write the workhorse swap proposal**, carrying the 32% throughput cost, the +5.68 GiB, and the unresolved Z1 over-claim as stated costs |
| `passed` 9-11 | mixed. No proposal; report the trade and let the owner decide whether document accuracy is worth general regression |
| `passed` <= 8 | document-specific. **Drop the swap case** and say so plainly |
| any `truncated` | N2's non-termination is not confined to one item; that is a reliability finding and it outranks the score |

### What this cannot establish

- 15 tasks, one run, one model. The core bank is saturated at the top - a cheap
  cloud model scored 14/15 - so it discriminates DOWNWARD only, which is exactly
  what is needed here and nothing more.
- Nothing about the abstention axis, still unrunnable (E120).
- Nothing about latency in production, where Nemotron's reasoning tokens cost
  far more wall clock than `tg128` alone suggests.

## E124 RESULT - Nemotron ties the workhorse on general work (2026-09-07)

`--task-set core`, 15 tasks, `--thinking default` (the only honest setting, per
E123).

| | workhorse (`e33-workhorse`) | **Nemotron (E124)** |
|---|---|---|
| total | 12/15 | **12/15** |
| easy | 2/2 | 2/2 |
| medium | 5/7 | **7/7** |
| hard | **5/6** | 3/6 |

| # | prediction | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HEADLINE: `passed` >= 12 - the swap gate | **held** - 12 |
| 2 | `passed` <= 14 | held - 12 |
| 3 | `truncated` = 0 | held - failures were `error` and `wrong_answer` |
| 4 | `no_code` and `transport_failed` both 0 | held |

**Same score, opposite profile.** Nemotron is steadier on routine work and
weaker at the top; the workhorse is the reverse. P3 holding matters: N2's
non-termination on l4 does not generalise to this bank.

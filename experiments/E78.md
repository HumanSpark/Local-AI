## E78 PRE-REGISTRATION - the L5 two-band bank: does the EVIDENCE band have any headroom at all? (2026-08-28)

**Registered while the arm it predicts is still running.** At the moment of
writing, `tools/run_ps_eval.py --tier l5 --label l5-qwen38` is in flight, its
output file `results/raw/l5-pilot-qwen38.json` does not exist, and its progress
log contains **zero** completed items. No answer, verdict or score from that arm
has been read. This entry is committed before the file appears so the order is
provable from git rather than asserted here.

**One arm has ALREADY RUN and nothing below is scored against it.** The
workhorse pilot is reported in the instrument section as CONSTRUCTION EVIDENCE,
because its numbers were seen before this registration was written. Treating
them as a scored result would be registering a prediction after reading its
answer, which is the failure this whole register exists to prevent.

### Why this experiment exists

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | neither headline question. This decides whether the *inference-architecture programme* has a measurable target at all on this hardware |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether the H1-H5 context-engineering branch has headroom to recover. Slice 1 argued the instruments are saturated; this measures it directly, on a bank built for the purpose |

### The instrument

`spikes/ps-eval/corpus_l5.py` + `spikes/ps-eval/questions_l5.py`, tier `l5`,
gated by `tools/validate_ps_eval_l5.py`. **24 items, 12 per band**, one
8,807-character closed-record pack of ten documents: a framework agreement with
an explicit precedence clause and counting conventions, three structurally
parallel call-off orders, a variation letter amending exactly one of them,
statutory extracts, a 2026 holiday calendar, an agreed chronology, an internal
note that contradicts the chronology and is expressly not agreed, and a
background note carrying a standstill agreement.

**The band is the design, and it is what makes this bank different from L4.**

| band | what is hard | what is handed over | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R** (12) | walking a period across a real calendar, composing two constraints | the clause, the document and the trigger date are NAMED IN THE QUESTION | F60 and E54: walked periods separated the arms 6/7, 4/7, 0/7 while static analysis separated nothing |
| **E** (12) | deciding which of four parallel instruments governs, reconciling an agreed record against a contradicting note, noticing the record is silent | nothing - the reasoning is one step | **this is the only band context engineering could move.** If it has no headroom, H1-H5 has nothing to recover |

Band R hands its evidence over deliberately, and the validator enforces it: an
item that required finding the clause AND walking the period would confound the
two bands, and the entire value of the tier is that it does not.

### The gate refused the bank six times, and every refusal was a real defect

Five at construction, one in the pilot. Recorded because the count is what the
gate is worth: six defects that would otherwise have reached a scored run.

| defect | why it mattered |
|---|---|
| `R11` was band R but named no clause | it required a search it was supposed to hand over - an E item wearing an R label |
| `R02`'s "+30 days" trap **equalled its own answer** | 31 May plus 30 days is 30 June, and so is the clamped one-month date. The trap could detect nothing. Removed, and the item's claim narrowed to the clamp alone |
| the shared decline vocabulary misses the plural present tense | *"the documents do not state"* is what a model actually says about a multi-document pack, and it would have scored as an **over-claim** - inverting the one number the underspecified items exist to produce |
| a decline parsed as "no" | *"does not state"* contains "not", so a decline scored **correct** on every yes_no item whose answer is no |
| the decline check ran before the positive match | a correct answer explaining a silence - *"CO-2 does not state a Notice Period, so the Framework default applies"* - was scored wrong |
| `E05`'s count alias missed a bare "2" | the fully correct `2; CO-1 and CO-3` scored `partial`. The alias is now a regex with a lookbehind, because a bare 2 would otherwise match the one inside "CO-2" and let an answer listing every document satisfy the count, breaking the posture check the item exists for |

**`DECLINE_PATTERNS` was NOT widened.** L2, L3 and L4 verdicts are banked
against it and changing it would silently restate them. L5 carries its own
detector that extends the shared one.

**Five synthetic respondents gate the bank** and all five signatures are exact:
`perfect` 24/24; `calendar_blind` exactly 12 `off_by_calendar`;
`never_declines` exactly 4 `over_claim`; `always_declines` exactly the 4
underspecified and **zero** over-claims; `lists_everything` unable to win either
exhaustive item.

## E78 RESULT - the reasoning band separates by 8 points, the evidence band by 2, and the best local model is already at its ceiling. PREDICTIONS 3 AND 4 FALSIFIED. (2026-08-28)

Three arms, 24 items, tier `l5`, `-c 16384`, `-fa on`, kernel `7.0.0-29-generic`.
**Rule 9 breached and acknowledged in every result file; all wall times VOID and
none is quoted.**

| arm | total | **band R** | **band E** | band R outcomes | band E outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-30B-A3B (production) | 13/24 | **3/12** | **10/12** | 3 correct, 7 wrong, 2 off_by_calendar | 10 correct, 1 wrong, 1 over_claim |
| Qwen3-8B (thinking on) | 16/24 | **5/12** | **11/12** | 5 correct, 3 wrong, 2 off_by_calendar, 2 truncated | 11 correct, 1 partial |
| Qwen3.8-27B `low` | 23/24 | **11/12** | **12/12** | 11 correct, 1 off_by_calendar | 12 correct |

**Separation across the three arms: band R 8 points, band E 2 points.** The
ordering is the same in both bands and matches E54's, so band E is not
insensitive to capability - it has roughly a quarter of the dynamic range.

### THE ANSWER

> **The evidence band is nearly exhausted before any intervention runs.** The
> production model already answers 10 of 12 evidence-limited items on a
> ten-document pack containing four parallel instruments, a variation letter,
> an internal note contradicting the agreed record and four absences. The best
> local model answers **12 of 12**. So the entire ceiling available to
> retrieval, reranking, small context models and evidence packs on a pack this
> size is **the two items the production model misses** - and the ceiling is
> demonstrably reachable, because another model already reaches it.

Stated with its interval rather than as a point: the production model's band E
is 10/12, **95% Wilson 55.2-95.3%**, and the headroom is 2/12, **95% Wilson
4.7-44.8%** (`wilson-v2-2026-08-20`). Twelve items cannot bound this tightly and
the entry says so before anyone quotes it.

### The tie-break fired, and it was written for exactly this

The registered rule: *"P2 holds and band E separates by exactly 2 -> ambiguous,
do NOT close H1-H5. Run Qwen3-8B, which E54 placed between the two arms at
11/15, and let the three-point ordering decide."*

That is what happened, and the flattering reading - *"band E barely moves, close
the branch"* - was available and is not taken. Qwen3-8B landed between the two
arms on both bands, 5 on R and 11 on E, preserving the ordering. What the third
point decides is that **band E is monotonic in capability and merely has a small
range**, rather than being a broken or insensitive band. That is the reading
that supports the answer above; a non-monotonic third point would have refused
it.

### Prediction 4 is falsified by MY DESIGN, not by the model

`R12` is the two-constraint composition item, built from E54's `N2` where both
capable models found each rule and dropped one. Qwen3.8 answered it correctly.

**The reason is in the question text.** `R12` tells the model that clause 3.4
and the Notice Period *"are cumulative"*. E54's `N2` did not: the model had to
notice that two constraints applied at all. Band R's own discipline - hand the
evidence over so the band stays clean - removed the composition difficulty along
with the search difficulty.

**So this bank does not currently test composition, and one item that claims to
is misdescribed.** The fix is not to weaken band R's discipline, which is what
keeps the two bands separable; it is a third band, or an `R12b` that hands over
both clauses and withholds the instruction to combine them. Recorded rather than
patched, because changing an item after seeing an arm's answer to it is how a
bank stops meaning anything.

### The decision rule's audit branch fired and found a defect in MY analysis tool

The rule said *"any format_error -> audit the grader before reading any score"*.
Two appeared on the 8B. They were not grader faults and not model faults:
`run_ps_eval.py` stores a truncated answer as `None`, and `tools/score_l5_bands.py`
re-graded `None` as `format_error`, discarding the runner's more specific
`truncated`. The 8B hit `finish_reason: length` at 4,096 tokens after **14,968
characters of reasoning** on `R10`, which is a token-budget outcome and needs a
different fix from an unusable answer shape.

`score_l5_bands.py` now preserves the outcomes the runner owns. **Reported before
the fix, the 8B would have carried two format_errors it never committed** - and
under this tier's own decision rule that would have sent an audit after the
grader instead of after the budget.

### Bank amendment, declared: R09's trap list missed the error that happened

The registered `off_by_calendar` traps for `R09` carried the sequential reading
and a 90-day count. **Both capable arms answered 31 July 2026** - the two-month
extension applied ALONE, with the parallel one-month period dropped - which was
not in the list, so the tier's headline diagnostic scored its own mechanism as a
generic `wrong`.

The trap is added. **It relabels R09 from `wrong` to `off_by_calendar` for
Qwen3.8 and for Qwen3-8B and moves no `correct` count on any arm**, which is why
it is a safe amendment to make after the fact and is declared here rather than
applied quietly. This is the third occurrence of the pattern in this repository:
L4 recorded the same thing after its own first run, and the lesson has now cost
three instruments.

### What this does NOT establish

- **Three local arms are not a field**, and no cloud arm ran.
- **The band E ceiling is a property of THIS pack** - 8,807 characters, ten
  documents. It says nothing about the 268,476-character `l` pack in
  `spikes/kw-eval/kw_corpus.py`, which has never been run and which is where a
  retrieval intervention would have the most to do. That run is gated on the
  >60 GiB regime and needs an attended window.
- **Twelve items per band.** Every band figure above carries an interval wider
  than the effect it measures, and the two-item headroom is 4.7-44.8%.
- **Nothing about latency**, and nothing about whether an intervention would
  actually recover the two items - only that two is what there is to recover.

### What this means for the programme, stated as a recommendation and not a decision

The plan's Phases 2 and 3 (H1 selected context, H2 small context model, H3
query-time memory, H5 evidence packs) are aimed at a band where the production
model scores 83% and the best local model scores 100%. The plan's own
"Material" success threshold is a 5-point gain; on a 12-item band that is 0.6
items. **The measurable target for that whole branch is two items, and a
different model already takes both.**

Phase 4 (H7 reasoning search) and H16 (tools) aim at a band where the production
model scores 25% and loses 8 points to a model in the same memory envelope.
`tools/date_calc.py` already exists and was built for exactly this. That is
where the headroom is.

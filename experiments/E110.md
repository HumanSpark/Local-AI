## E110 PRE-REGISTRATION - where does the CONTROL score on the new bank? (2026-09-06)

**Registered before the bank has ever been served.** `results/raw/e110` does not exist.

### Why this experiment exists

`expert_tasks_v2.py` has 7 tasks, each proved solvable by a reference solution
and discriminating against a NAIVE one (`tools/validate_expert_tasks_v2.py`).
**That proves nothing about where a real model scores.** F145's rule, applied to
our own instrument: *before comparing two models, state where the control is
expected to score; if the answer is at ceiling, the comparison cannot run yet.*

A bank whose control scores 7/7 is exactly the failure that made L5 and the
coding core useless (F142). Discriminating against a deliberately-wrong solution
is a much weaker property than discriminating between two competent models, and
conflating them is how three instruments ended up saturated.

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | the instrument itself - whether the bank built to fix saturation is saturated |
| **uncertainty reduced** | where the incumbent lands, which sets whether the bank can be used at all |
| **decision that changes** | whether to keep writing tasks in this style, or make them harder |

### Design

One arm. `Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M`, `--task-set expert2`, `-c 32768 --max-tokens
24576 --thinking medium`, build `b10435`, quiet GPU - **identical to E104 and
E105**, so the number is directly comparable to the incumbent's 7/8 on the
original expert tier.

### Predictions, each naming the FIELD it is scored against

| # | prediction | scored against |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HEADLINE: the bank is not saturated** | incumbent `passed` **<= 5** of 7 |
| 2 | it is not impossible either | incumbent `passed` **>= 2** of 7 |
| 3 | the laziness contract is the hardest | `stream_dedupe` outcome is not `pass` |
| 4 | the complexity gate bites | `interval_cover` is `fail` or `timeout`, not `pass` |
| 5 | the harness holds | `no_code` and `transport_failed` both **0** |

**P1 is registered in the direction that costs the most to be wrong about.** A
7/7 means four hours of task-writing produced another saturated bank, and I would
rather find that now than after using it to judge a model.

### Registered decision rule

| observed | action |
|---|---|
| 2-5 of 7 | **the bank works.** Continue writing tasks in this style toward 30 |
| **6 or 7 of 7** | **saturated. STOP writing tasks in this style** - the naive-arm test is necessary and NOT sufficient, and the recipe needs a harder standard before more items are written |
| 0 or 1 of 7 | too hard to discriminate at this capability level. The tasks are not wrong, but they measure a different band and must be labelled as such |
| any `no_code` | prompt defect, not capability - the task text does not determine the answer, and that is a bug in the task |

### What this cannot establish

- **One model, one run, 7 items.** It locates the control, nothing more.
- **A pass here is "passes hidden tests", not "good code".**
- Nothing about the other two challengers; they are not run.

### THE ANSWER

> **Passing the naive-arm test is necessary and nowhere near sufficient.** Every
> expert2 task was proved to defeat a deliberately-wrong implementation. A
> competent model does not write the deliberately-wrong implementation - it
> writes the correct one. The bank measures the gap between RIGHT and OBVIOUSLY
> WRONG, where a discriminating bank must measure the gap between RIGHT and
> SUBTLY WRONG.
>
> **The token figure is the tell.** These tasks cost the incumbent a quarter of
> what the original tier costs it. They are fiddly to SPECIFY, not hard to
> SOLVE - `round_half_even` is exacting until you reach for `Decimal`, and the
> model did.

### Scoring

| # | prediction | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HEADLINE: bank is not saturated (<= 5 of 7)** | **FALSIFIED** - 7 |
| 2 | not impossible either (>= 2) | HELD - trivially |
| 3 | `stream_dedupe` is the hardest, does not pass | **FALSIFIED** - passed in 1,480 tokens |
| 4 | the complexity gate bites `interval_cover` | **FALSIFIED** - passed in 2,336 tokens |
| 5 | `no_code` + `transport_failed` = 0 | HELD - 0 |

### The registered branch fired

Registered: *"6 or 7 of 7 -> saturated. STOP writing tasks in this style - the
naive-arm test is necessary and NOT sufficient, and the recipe needs a harder
standard before more items are written."*

**Stopped at 7 tasks rather than continuing to 30.** The 23 unwritten tasks are
the value of having registered this before writing them.

### What the recipe needs, decided by the owner 2026-09-06

Every task must additionally defeat a **competent-but-subtly-wrong** solution -
one a good engineer would plausibly write - not only a strawman. The subtle
error is to be **sourced from failures we have actually measured**, not from
imagination: `sliding_median`, which defeats every model and every quant tested;
F89's boundary error on gpt-oss-120b; F92's two models miscounting the same
deadline identically.

That last constraint exists because my sense of "plausible" produced seven tasks
the model passed. The calibration, not the authoring, did all the work.

### What this cannot establish

- **One model, one run, 7 items.** It locates the control and nothing more.
- The tasks are not WRONG - they are valid, tested and self-contained. They
  measure a capability band this model is already above.
- Nothing about whether they would discriminate BELOW this band; a weaker model
  might well fail several, which is a different bank for a different purpose.

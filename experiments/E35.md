## E35: does the flat SPEED curve come with a flat QUALITY curve? (2026-08-15, pre-registered before any run)

**The tension this exists to resolve.** F62 found Qwen3.8-27B's generation
speed barely decays with depth (-3.6% d0->d8192, flattest measured here), and
attributed it structurally: `full_attention_interval = 4`, so only 1 layer in
4 grows a KV cache. But the OTHER three layers are Gated DeltaNet - linear
attention with a fixed-size recurrent state, i.e. they COMPRESS history rather
than retaining it. Exact-needle retrieval is precisely the task that
compression should hurt.

So the flat speed curve and good long-context recall may be the same mechanism
paying off, or they may be a TRADE: cheap depth bought by throwing away detail.
Nothing in results/ goes past 32K, and this model claims 262,144.

**Config.** `tools/run_longctx_eval.py`, llama-server b10435, `-np 1`,
`-c = target + 4096` (F20: -c is not divided at np 1), temperature 0 (F59).
Corpus is real project prose sized by the model's OWN tokenizer via
`/tokenize` - never chars/4, per the CLAUDE.md rule that technical text runs
~40% over that estimate. Five sentinel facts (nonsense name + 6-digit number,
unable to occur naturally) planted at 5/25/50/75/95% depth in ONE corpus per
length, so all five questions share a prefix and the prompt cache pays the
prefill once. Grading is exact substring - no grader artifact possible.

**Lengths:** 8,192 / 32,768 / 65,536 / 131,072. **Arms:** Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M
and Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 (control).

**Predictions (registered before the run):**

1. **Qwen3.8 recall by length:** 8K **5/5**; 32K **5/5**; 64K **3-5/5**;
   128K **2-4/5**.
2. **Depth profile: middle depths are weakest.** The 25/50/75% needles fail
   before the 5% and 95% ones. Classic lost-in-the-middle, which state
   compression should amplify rather than soften.
3. **DISCRIMINATING CLAIM: at 128K, Qwen3.8's recall does NOT exceed the
   control's by more than 1 needle.** Reasoning: 3 layers in 4 carry a
   compressed recurrent state, so the hybrid's depth advantage is in COST, not
   in fidelity. **Falsifier: if Qwen3.8 scores 5/5 at 128K, compression costs
   nothing for exact retrieval at this scale and the hybrid is strictly better
   on both axes** - which would be the strongest single result of the night.
4. **Harness check, not a model claim: the first question at 128K takes
   400-700s (prefill at the measured 283 t/s pp), and questions 2-5 take under
   60s** because the prompt cache serves the shared prefix. If 2-5 do NOT
   speed up, the cache is not working, the design assumption is void, and no
   timing from this experiment may be quoted.

**Not predicted:** the control's absolute scores. Its effective context
handling at 128K is unmeasured here and guessing would be theatre.

**Amendments:** none.

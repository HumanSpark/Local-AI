### SATURATION VERDICT: CONTINUE

The rule halts only if every arm is perfect on the first ten. No arm is. L1's
12/12 ceiling is not reproduced and the remaining thirty items are worth running.

### The crossed net is +0 for gpt-oss, and reporting that alone would be wrong

| item | subset | no-tools | tools | tool calls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `B05` | boundary | **0** | **2** | 0 -> 6 |
| `A04` | ambiguity | **2** | **0** | 0 -> **0** |

**+2 where the tool is relevant, -2 where it is not, netting to zero.** A
single crossed figure would have read "tools are neutral for gpt-oss" and
concealed both halves. This is what the subset decomposition is for.

**`B05` is H6 demonstrated as cleanly as it gets.** Without tools gpt-oss
DECLINED the item: *"the material does not provide enough information to
determine the final deadline, as it lacks the number of days in February 2024
(i.e. whether it is a leap year)."* It refused a calendar question for want of
deterministic calendar knowledge. With the tools it answered correctly - 1 April
2024 - in six calls.

**`A04` reproduces F94's mechanism on a third instrument, and again with no tool
call.** Without tools the answer identified the gap (*"is not shown to have been
provided"*); with tools it flatly asserted the sign-off *"was not provided"* - a
claim about a fact the record does not establish. **Zero tool calls in both
conditions.** Offering the tools changed an answer on an item where the tool was
never used, which is exactly the serving-configuration effect F94 recorded.

**Arm B's gain runs the other way and is worth noting.** `B04` moved from
`fail_token_cap` to a correct answer once tools were offered: the tool
short-circuited the reasoning that had been consuming the whole 5,120-token
budget. Delegation bought completion, not just accuracy.

### Arm C

19/20, the single drop being `B04` at **1a**: it ended the 15-business-day cure
period on Tuesday 5 November instead of Wednesday 6 November, then counted
forward from a day early to 16 November against a key of 17 November. Right rule,
off-by-one arithmetic - the F92/F93 class reproducing on a purpose-built item.

### Two limits on this cell

**The crossed factor is TWO-ARM, not three.** Arm C runs through Claude Code
subagents, which cannot be handed an OpenAI-shaped `tools` schema the way
llama-server can, so no `armC / tools` cell exists. The crossing is measurable on
the local arms only, and any report of it must say so rather than implying three.

**No judge was needed.** Zero cells across all five runs required one - the
mechanical scorer settled every outcome and every citation. If that holds across
the full 40 the judge question never binds.

### The instrument discriminates on BOTH axes, which L3-hard never did

Ambiguity: frontier 40 > Qwen3.8 38/37 > gpt-oss 32. Boundary with tools:
Qwen3.8 **40** > gpt-oss 38 > frontier 34 > Qwen3.8 unaided 32. **No ceiling, no
floor, and a different ordering on each half.** That is what E65's saturated tier
could not produce.

### The crossed factor, corrected for B20

| model | total | boundary | ambiguity |
|---|---|---|---|
| gpt-oss-120b | +2 | +2 | 0 |
| Qwen3.8-27B | **+7** | **+8** | **-1** |

**The calculator takes Qwen3.8 to a perfect 40/40 on arithmetic** and costs it
one point on ambiguity (`A13` moves 2 -> 1b). Seven of the eight boundary gains
are cells it previously got wrong or could not finish; `B04` moves from
`fail_token_cap` to correct, which is delegation buying COMPLETION rather than
accuracy - the tool short-circuited reasoning that had been consuming the whole
5,120-token budget.

### The headline comparison is NOT like-for-like and must not be reported as one

Qwen3.8 + calculator at 77/80 sits above the frontier arm at 74/80, but **the
frontier arm has no tools condition and cannot have one**: it is reached through
Claude Code subagents, which cannot be handed an OpenAI-shaped `tools` schema. So
that line compares a local model WITH a calculator against a frontier model
WITHOUT one.

**On the fair comparison - no arm has tools - the frontier arm leads: 74, 70,
68.** What is established is that a calculator closes most of that gap and
reverses it on the arithmetic half. What is NOT established is what the frontier
arm would score with the same help.

### B20, and what it says about our own test writing

The original B20 stated a no-roll rule but no month-clamp rule, so 29 Feb 2024 +
12 months had no resolution in the text. The frontier arm declined it on exactly
that ground. Amended and re-run on all five cells:

| arm / condition | original | amended |
|---|---|---|
| gpt-oss-120b / no-tools | 0 | **2** |
| gpt-oss-120b / tools | 2 | 2 |
| Qwen3.8-27B / no-tools | 0 | **1b** |
| Qwen3.8-27B / tools | 2 | 2 |
| frontier / no-tools | 0 | **2** |

Every arm that failed the original passes or improves on the amended item, and
no arm that passed regressed. **The item was the fault, and the arm that caught
it was the one the item scored zero.**

### Limits

Five of forty cells could not be settled mechanically (`A06`, `A08`, `A10`,
`A11`, `A13`) and are flagged rather than guessed; they need a judge that is not
any arm's own model, which is still unavailable. Forty items cannot resolve small
differences - a gap of a few points is inside the noise this design can see. The
frontier arm is version-unpinned. And the 5,120-token cap is a declared serving
condition: Qwen3.8 lost two cells to it unaided and one with tools.

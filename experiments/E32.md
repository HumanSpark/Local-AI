## E32: concurrency sweep, Qwen3.8-27B vs MoE workhorse (2026-08-15, pre-registered before any run)

**Why this exists:** on 2026-08-15 an operator-launched triplet and a
session-launched one collided by accident. Two fully-contended independent
streams measured 6.84 and 6.83 t/s (agreement 0.15%) against a clean solo
12.607, i.e. **aggregate 13.67 = 1.081x solo**. Concurrency headroom that
large should never be discovered by accident, which is what this experiment
and the accompanying harness rule exist to prevent.

**Config.** `tools/serve_bench.py`, llama-server b10435
(`SPARKBENCH_SERVER_BIN` override), `-np` in {1,2,4,8}, `--ctx-per-slot 4096`
(so `-c` = slots x 4096, per F20 the total is DIVIDED by slots), KV f16,
`--gen-tokens 128` for tg128 semantics, 45s measured window. Relay down under
the maintenance flag throughout. Rule 3 full-offload gate enforced by the
harness itself.

**Arms.** Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M (dense, 15.65 GiB) and
Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507-Q4_K_M (MoE, 17.28 GiB) as control.

**Predictions (registered before the run):**

1. **Qwen3.8 slots=1 aggregate reproduces the llama-bench triplet**, 12.3-12.9
   t/s. This is a harness-agreement check: two different instruments on the
   same config must agree, or one of them is wrong (measurement integrity,
   not a model claim).
2. **Qwen3.8 scales SUPERLINEARLY in relative terms versus the MoE.** Reason:
   dense single-stream generation is weight-bandwidth-bound - all 15.82 GB is
   re-read per token - so batching amortises that read across B tokens. The
   MoE already has low active bytes per token, so it has less to amortise.
   Registered bands, relative to each model's own slots=1:
   - Qwen3.8 @2: **1.6-2.0x** | @4: **2.6-4.0x** | @8: **3.5-6.0x**
   - Qwen3-30B-A3B @8: **1.5-2.2x** (anchored on the recorded 193.5 agg at 16
     slots vs 92.28 single = 2.1x)
3. **Discriminating claim: Qwen3.8's @8 multiplier EXCEEDS the workhorse's @8
   multiplier.** If false - if the dense model batches no better than the MoE
   - the bandwidth-amortisation explanation is wrong and the +8.1% from the
   accident needs another mechanism.
4. **No prediction registered for TTFT or the knee location.** F17 puts
   gpt-oss-20b's knee at ~4 slots, but that is a different architecture class
   and guessing here would be theatre.

**Falsifier for the whole experiment:** if slots=1 disagrees with the triplet
by more than F15's +/-1.5%, the harness and llama-bench are measuring
different things and NO scaling number from this run may be quoted until that
is resolved.

**Amendments:** none. Any amendment must be recorded here before the affected run.

### The falsifier did NOT fire, and the reason is the measurement rule, not the model

The registered falsifier was: *if slots=1 disagrees with the llama-bench triplet
by more than F15's +/-1.5%, the two instruments are measuring different things
and no scaling number may be quoted.*

**`aggregate` and llama-bench's `tg128` are not the same quantity.** Rule 10
names the pair: aggregate charges TTFT and queueing, capacity is steady-state
generation. llama-bench reports the second. The comparable figure is
`mean_stream_gen_tps` = **12.40** against the triplet's **12.607**, a
**-1.48%** difference, inside F15's +/-1.5% single-run noise.

**So prediction 1 named the wrong field and scores FALSIFIED; the harness
agreement it was actually testing HOLDS.** That is the "compare like-for-like"
rule arriving as a scoring outcome rather than as advice, and it is the reason
the standing rule now requires each prediction to name the field it is scored
against - a rule written after E64 for exactly this shape.

### What survives

The mechanism direction was right and its magnitude was overestimated. Dense
Qwen3.8 does batch better than the sparse MoE in relative terms (2.84x against
2.20x at 8 slots), which supports the bandwidth-amortisation explanation for
the +8.1% seen in the 2026-08-15 accidental collision. But the registered bands
of 3.5-6.0x were roughly double what the hardware delivers, and any planning
figure taken from them would have been wrong.

**Evidence:** `results/raw/e32-20260815-022224-*.json` (10 files, 180s window,
`under_sampled` false throughout), `results/raw/e32-20260815-021141-*.json`
(45s window, kept, two cells flagged).

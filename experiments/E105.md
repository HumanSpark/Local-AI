### Why this experiment exists

E104 established the bottom of the ladder: `GSQ-RCO IQ3_S` scores **4/8** where
`Q4_K_M` scores **7/8**, for the same tokens. **That answers "does going down
cost?" and says nothing about "does going up buy?"** - and the second question
is the one with a production consequence, because this box has 128 GiB and a
27 GiB Q8_0 fits resident where a 24 GiB-VRAM box could never hold it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **which success question** | production serving - whether the `smart` slot is under-quantised |
| **uncertainty reduced** | whether Q4_K_M is at the flat part of the quant curve or below it |
| **decision that changes** | if Q8_0 beats Q4_K_M on a discriminating bank, the `smart` slot should go UP, not stay |

### Provenance

Raised by a second community report running **INT8 W8A8** on an A40 and calling
it "the GOAT". **That stack is not testable here** - vLLM is not viable on
gfx1151 (F48), `compressed-tensors` W8A8 is not a GGUF format, and FLASHINFER is
CUDA-only. Q8_0 GGUF is the nearest thing this box can actually serve, and the
question it answers - *is 8-bit better than 4-bit here* - is the transferable
part of that report.

## E105 RESULT - Q4_K_M is the knee of the quantisation curve. All five predictions HELD. (2026-09-06)

Expert tier, 8 tasks, `-c 32768 --max-tokens 24576 --thinking medium`, build
`b10435`, kernel `7.0.0-29`, quiet GPU. Comparator is E104's incumbent arm from
the same session, build, kernel and config.

| quant | on disk | pass | tokens | wall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GSQ-RCO-IQ3_S` (E104) | 11.29 GiB | **4/8** | 75,307 | 4,951 s |
| **`Q4_K_M` (incumbent, E104)** | **15.66 GiB** | **7/8** | 74,796 | 6,206 s |
| `Q8_0` | 27.05 GiB | **7/8** | 72,803 | **9,614 s** |

Both 7/8 arms fail the SAME task, `sliding_median`, with `wrong_answer`.

### THE ANSWER

> **Going up buys nothing; going down costs three of eight.** Q8_0 matches
> Q4_K_M exactly - same score, same failing task, 2.7% fewer tokens - for
> **11.4 GiB more memory and 55% more wall clock**. We are not under-quantised,
> and the ladder is not flat: it has a knee, and Q4_K_M sits on it.

| 1 | **HEADLINE: Q8_0 does not beat Q4_K_M** (<= 0) | **HELD** - 7 vs 7, difference 0 |
| 2 | Q8_0 is not worse either (>= 6) | **HELD** - 7 |

P3 matters more than its size suggests: `sliding_median` fails at 3-bit, 4-bit

artefact**, and it contributes no separation anywhere on this tier.

### The registered branch

Registered: *"P1 holds and P2 holds -> the curve is flat from Q4 to Q8. Q4_K_M is
the right operating point; the 11.4 GiB saving over Q8_0 is free. No production
change."* **That is the branch. Production is unchanged and correct.**

### What is NOT claimed

- **Eight items, one run per arm.** A tie at 7/8 on n=8 cannot exclude a
  difference smaller than the bank resolves.
- **Nothing about INT8 W8A8**, the community claim that prompted this. Different
  format, different hardware, different kernel path; vLLM is not viable on
  gfx1151 (F48). Stated at registration and restated here.
- **One quant per rung.** UD-Q8_K_L and UD-Q8_K_XL mix precisions per tensor and
  were deliberately not used - they would confound "is 8-bit better" with "is
  unsloth's allocation better".
- **The wall-clock figures are valid** - quiet GPU, unlike E109's.

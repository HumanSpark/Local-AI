## E104 PRE-REGISTRATION - the expert tier as the harder instrument, after L5 saturated (2026-09-04)

**Registered before any arm runs.** `results/raw/e104` does not exist.

### Why this experiment exists

F142 retired the L5 bank for model comparison: three models, including a 3-bit
quant and a different base model family, all scored 23/24 with the same failing
item. The core coding tier is at ceiling too - the E102 second bank returned
**15/15** for the incumbent tonight. **Two saturated instruments cannot decide
whether the GSQ-RCO quant should displace anything**, which is the open
question, and adding a fourth arm to them would measure nothing.

### The instrument already existed, and it discriminates

`spikes/coding-eval/expert_tasks.py`, 8 tasks, `--task-set expert`. Built
2026-08-15 for precisely this failure ("the core set's hard tier does not
discriminate at the top"), and built with the property the saturated banks lack:
**each test block was checked to FAIL a plausible naive solution**, so a task
everything passes was rejected at construction.

Banked spread on it, which is why it is the right choice:

| arm | effort | score |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M | `medium` | 7/8 |
| Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M | `low` | 5/8 |
| Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M | `off` | 3/8 |
| Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B | default | 3/8 |
| KAT-Coder-V2.5 | `off` | 2/8 |
| KAT-Coder-V2.5 | default | 0/8 |

**0 to 7 of 8 is real dynamic range.** No new bank is built here; searching
before building is the rule and the search found one.

### Config, matched to E42 and NOT to tonight's other arms

`-c 32768`, `--max-tokens 24576`, `--thinking medium`, `--task-set expert`,
build `b10435`, quiet GPU. Deliberately matched to the banked E42 run so the
control is comparable.

⚠️ **E42 ran on kernel `7.0.0-28`; tonight is `7.0.0-29`.** F57 makes the kernel
a live bench variable, so **the incumbent is re-run in this session as a control
rather than compared against the banked 7/8 across a kernel boundary.**

### The three arms

| arm | model | effort |
|---|---|---|
| control | `Qwen3.8-27B-Q4_K_M` | `medium` |

### Predictions, each naming the FIELD it is scored against

| # | prediction | scored against |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the control reproduces across the kernel boundary | incumbent `pass` **>= 6 of 8**, and `sliding_median` is among its failures |
| 2 | **DISCRIMINATING: this bank is NOT saturated** | max `pass` minus min `pass` across the three arms **>= 2 of 8** |
| 3 | the quant does not degrade | incumbent `pass` minus quant `pass` **<= 1** |
| 4 | ThinkingCap does not beat our tuned incumbent | ThinkingCap `pass` **<=** incumbent `pass` |
| 5 | ThinkingCap costs more tokens than the tuned incumbent | ThinkingCap total `completion_tokens` **>** incumbent's |
| 6 | the harness holds | `transport_failed` + `no_code` **= 0** across all 24 |

**P2 is the one that decides what happens next**, and it is a prediction about
the INSTRUMENT rather than the models. It is registered because two banks
saturated tonight and a third would stop this being bad luck.

### Registered decision rule

| observed | action |
|---|---|
| P2 holds and P3 holds | the quant survives a discriminating bank. **That is the evidence a `smart`-slot proposal needs**, and it goes to sparkrouter as a proposal (F39: `serving.conf` has one writer and it is not this repo) |
| P2 holds and P3 FAILS | the quant degrades where it counts. The L5 tie was the bank's blindness, not equivalence. Recommend AGAINST the swap and say the earlier null was uninformative |
| **P2 FAILS - all three within 1 of each other** | **the expert tier is saturated too. Three banks is a pattern, not luck: STOP comparing and BUILD harder items**, with these three nulls as the stated justification. No recommendation about any model is made on saturated instruments |
| P1 fails | the control did not reproduce across the kernel boundary. Nothing may be concluded about the challengers; investigate the kernel/build first |

### What this cannot establish

- **Eight items.** A 2-point spread on n=8 is a wide interval and any figure is
  quoted with it.
- **The arms are not effort-matched** (ThinkingCap has no dial, F143).
- **A pass is "passes hidden tests", not "good code".**


## E104 RESULT - the bank with headroom separates the three models by SIX points, and the quant recommendation INVERTS. PREDICTION 3 FALSIFIED. (2026-09-05)

Three arms, 8 expert tasks, `-c 32768`, `--max-tokens 24576`, build `b10435`,
kernel `7.0.0-29-generic`, quiet GPU throughout.

| arm | effort | pass | tokens | wall |

### THE ANSWER

> **Every model spent the same tokens. They differ by six of eight.** The three
> arms consumed 74,796, 75,307 and 77,861 completion tokens - within 4% of each
> other - and scored 7, 4 and 1. This is not a budget effect, a truncation
> effect or a configuration effect. It is capability, and three other banks
> could not see any of it.

### The control reproduced ACROSS A KERNEL BOUNDARY

| run | kernel | date | pass | only failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E42 | `7.0.0-28` | 2026-08-15 | 7/8 | `sliding_median` |
| E104 | `7.0.0-29` | 2026-09-05 | **7/8** | **`sliding_median`** |

Same score, same failing item, three weeks and a kernel bump apart. F57 makes
the kernel a live bench variable that `results/` does not record, so this was a
real risk. **P1 HELD**, and it is what licenses the two challenger comparisons.

### Scoring

| 6 | `transport_failed` + `no_code` = 0 | **HELD** - 0; all 12 failures are genuine `wrong_answer` (9) or `error` (3) |

### The registered branch, and what it forbids

Registered: *"P2 holds and P3 FAILS -> the quant degrades where it counts. The
L5 tie was the bank's blindness, not equivalence. Recommend AGAINST the swap and
say the earlier null was uninformative."*

**That is the branch. Recommendation: do NOT swap the `smart` slot to
GSQ-RCO IQ3_S, and do NOT swap it to ThinkingCap.** The incumbent survives both
challenges.

### The same three models, on four banks

| bank | incumbent | quant | ThinkingCap | spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L5 knowledge (24) | 23/24 | 23/24 | 23/24 | **0** |
| coding core (15) | 15/15 | 14/15 | - | 1 |

**The instrument decided the conclusion, not the models.** Three banks said "no
difference detected" about a pair that differ by three of eight. Had E102 been
the last word - a tie, 34% faster, 4.7 GiB smaller - the defensible-looking move
was to propose the swap, and it would have been wrong. The only thing that
prevented it was the pre-registered rule requiring a second bank before any
recommendation, written before the null appeared.

### What is NOT claimed

- **Eight items, one run per arm.** The 7/4/1 ordering is clear; the exact gaps
  are not bounded at n=8 and no interval is quoted because one run cannot supply
  one.
- **The arms are not effort-matched** (ThinkingCap has no dial, F143). Its 1/8
  includes that asymmetry - though it spent MORE tokens than the incumbent, so
  the result is not explained by under-thinking.
- **One quant of eight** in the ISTA repo; **one third-party requant** for
  ThinkingCap, MTP head not enabled.
- **`sliding_median` fails for all three** and is the tier's hardest item; it
  contributes no separation.
